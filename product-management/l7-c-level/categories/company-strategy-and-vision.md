# Company Strategy and Vision

- Setting product vision at company scale and tying it to the company's theory of winning
- Market creation vs. market capture; timing and sequencing of bets
- Portfolio strategy across business lines, including what to divest
- Platform, ecosystem and partnership strategy
- Reading structural change — regulation, technology shifts, distribution shifts — early
- Capital allocation: where the next 100 people and the next budget go
- M&A: thesis, diligence, integration, and knowing when not to buy
- Long-range planning without pretending to certainty
- Narrative: articulating the strategy so employees, customers and investors all recognise it
- Deciding when the company's strategy itself is wrong
