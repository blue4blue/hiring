# Business Model and P&L

- Owning company-level product economics: revenue, gross margin, contribution margin
- The growth model of the whole business and its binding constraint
- Pricing and monetisation strategy across the portfolio
- Trade-offs between growth, profitability and cash
- Cost structure: infrastructure, support, ops, and product decisions that drive them
- New revenue lines: validation, incubation and graduation criteria
- Enterprise, SMB, self-serve and marketplace motions in one company
- Regulatory, legal and risk exposure created by product choices
- Investor-grade forecasting and the discipline of hitting a number
- Reading the financial statements of your own company critically
