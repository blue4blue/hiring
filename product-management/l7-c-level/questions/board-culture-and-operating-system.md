# Board, Culture and Operating System

* Describe your relationship with your last CEO. Where did you disagree, and how did that resolve?
* Tell me about presenting to a board. What did you show, and what did they push back on?
* Tell me about delivering bad news — a missed number, a failed launch, a breach.
* How is a product decision made in your company today? Draw the actual path.
* What is the product operating system you would install here in year one?
* How do you keep customer contact alive at executive level?
* Tell me about a crisis you led through.
* What has your company refused to build on ethical grounds? Were you the one who said no?
* How would you handle a peer executive who consistently undermines product?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you say in the room, not what you wished you had said?"
* "How did the board's confidence change afterwards, and how did you know?"
