# Business Model and P&L

* What was the size and shape of the P&L you influenced? What were you accountable for?
* What is the binding constraint on our growth, based on what you can see from outside?
* Tell me about a monetisation change at company scale. What were the second-order effects?
* How do you balance growth against profitability when the board wants both?
* Describe a new revenue line you incubated. What were the graduation criteria?
* How do you decide when to kill a business line that is still growing but structurally weak?
* Tell me about a product decision that created serious regulatory or legal exposure.
* What number did you commit to publicly, and did you hit it?
* How do you manage infrastructure or support cost through product decisions?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What were the actual numbers, and what was your personal contribution to them?"
* "What did the CFO say about that, and how did you resolve it?"
