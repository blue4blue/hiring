# Org and Talent

* Describe the largest product organisation you have led: size, shape, geography.
* How many VPs or Heads of Product have you hired? How did they work out?
* Tell me about your best hire and your worst. What was the difference in your process?
* How do you build a bench so that you are not the single point of failure?
* How do you calibrate levels and compensation across several business lines?
* Tell me about an executive you had to exit. How did you handle it?
* How do you partner with a CTO who disagrees with you about ownership?
* How do you scale culture when the org triples?
* What decisions did you deliberately give away as the org grew?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who from your org is now running product somewhere else?"
* "What was the first signal that hire was not working, and how long did you wait?"
