# Company Strategy and Vision

* Tell me the product vision you set at company level and how it changed the company.
    * How did you know it had landed?
* What was your theory of why your company would win? Was it right?
* Describe the hardest capital allocation decision you made.
    * What did you starve, and what happened to it?
* Tell me about a structural shift in your market you saw before others did — or missed.
* Walk me through a divestment, shutdown or exit you led.
* Have you led an acquisition? Talk me through the thesis and the integration.
    * What did you get wrong about the integration?
* You have read about our company. What is your first hypothesis about our strategy, and what would you want to test in 90 days?
* When did you tell your CEO the strategy was wrong? What happened?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the sacrifice, and who inside the company paid it?"
* "What would have made you abandon that thesis?"
