# Rubric — L6 Head of Product / Director / VP Product

A Head of Product owns the product organisation for a business line. They are assessed
on **the system they build**: strategy, org, operating model and commercial results —
not on features they personally shipped.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategy & vision** | Aggregated team roadmaps | Sets a business-line strategy with real sacrifice and sequencing | Found a structural advantage others had missed and built on it |
| **Org design & hiring** | Hires to fill seats | Shapes the org to the strategy; hires and levels consistently | Built a bench; their alumni run product orgs elsewhere |
| **Operating model** | Imported a framework wholesale | Designed a cadence fit for this company's stage and killed what didn't fit | Turned a slow org into a fast one and can show how |
| **Business & commercial** | Product metrics only | Owns a P&L or revenue line and forecasts credibly | Changed the business model or economics through product |
| **Executive presence** | Long, unfocused updates | Answer first, evidence behind it, honest about risk | Trusted by CEO/board as a source of truth, including bad news |
| **Talent judgement** | Avoids hard people calls | Manages performance fairly and promptly | Raises the average level of every team they touch |

## Must have

* Has managed managers, not only individual contributors.
* Has owned a revenue or P&L number and been held to it.
* Has designed an org and can explain the shape against the strategy.
* Has delivered bad news to an executive team or board and kept credibility.

## Nice to have

* Experience at both our current stage and the stage we are heading into.
* Domain or business-model familiarity.
* Experience integrating an acquisition or standing up a new business line.

## Red flags

* Talks about the org exclusively in headcount terms.
* Cannot name a single person they developed into a leader.
* Every improvement was a reorg.
* Strategy is a list of initiatives with no sacrifice in it.
* Blames the previous leadership for everything they inherited.

## First 90 days if hired

Diagnose the org and the strategy in writing, make the two or three people calls that
are already obvious, fix the single worst part of the operating model, and commit to one
number for the following quarter.
