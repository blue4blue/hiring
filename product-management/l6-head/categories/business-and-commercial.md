# Business and Commercial

- Owning a P&L or a major revenue line
- Forecasting, budgeting and defending the plan
- Growth model: acquisition, activation, retention, monetisation, referral
- CAC, LTV, payback, churn and net revenue retention
- Working with sales, marketing and customer success as partners not requesters
- Enterprise vs. self-serve motions and the product consequences of each
- Contracts, SLAs, and commitments product has to honour
- Build vs. buy vs. partner vs. acquire; basic M&A literacy
- Cost management: infrastructure, support load, ops per unit
- Board- and exec-level reporting on product performance
