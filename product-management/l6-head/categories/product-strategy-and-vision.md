# Product Strategy and Vision

- Setting a product vision for a business line and making it believable
- Translating company strategy into product strategy and back again
- Market analysis: TAM, segments, structural advantage, timing
- Choosing the battles: two or three bets per year, not ten
- Portfolio balance: core, adjacent, transformational
- Platform and ecosystem strategy; partnerships and integrations
- Pricing, packaging and monetisation strategy at the business-line level
- Competitive response: when to react and when to ignore
- Communicating vision internally and externally without over-promising
- Revisiting strategy on a cadence, and admitting when it failed
