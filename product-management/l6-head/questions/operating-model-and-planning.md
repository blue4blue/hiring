# Operating Model and Planning

* Describe your planning process end to end. How long does it take and who is involved?
    * What percentage of the year does planning consume?
* What rituals did you kill, and what happened?
* How are decision rights defined in your org? What comes to you?
* How do you make sure teams are empowered rather than order-takers, while still delivering commitments?
* How does an idea from an engineer reach the roadmap?
* What does your exec reporting look like? What is in it, and what did you remove?
* A launch goes badly and the org blames another function. What do you do?
* How do you run a post-launch review that people are honest in?
* How do you know your operating model is working?
