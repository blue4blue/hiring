# Product Strategy and Vision

* Describe the product vision you set in your last role. How did you arrive at it?
    * How did you know people believed it?
* How did your product strategy connect to the company strategy? Where did they conflict?
* What were your two or three bets last year? What did you say no to in order to fund them?
* Tell me about a competitive move you deliberately ignored.
* How do you decide between deepening the core and opening a new adjacent line?
* Walk me through a monetisation or packaging strategy you owned.
* Look at our product and market. What would your first strategic hypothesis be?
    * What would you need in your first 60 days to confirm or kill it?
* Tell me about a strategy of yours that failed. What did you do when it became clear?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the structural advantage — why you, and not a competitor?"
* "What did you personally write, and who did you have to convince?"
