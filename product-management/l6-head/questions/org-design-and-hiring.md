# Org Design and Hiring

* Describe the org you built. How was it shaped, and why that shape?
    * What would you change if you did it again?
* How do you decide the PM-to-engineer ratio?
* How many people have you hired? How many did you get wrong, and what was the pattern?
* Walk me through your interview loop design for a senior PM.
* How do you run promotion calibration so it stays fair?
* Tell me about managing someone out. What did you do first?
* You inherit a team with two strong PMs, three weak ones and no bar. What do you do in the first quarter?
* When is a reorg the right answer, and what is its real cost?
* How do you keep your best people?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Where are those people now, and would they work for you again?"
* "What behaviour did you tolerate that you should not have?"
