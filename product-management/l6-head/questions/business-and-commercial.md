# Business and Commercial

* What P&L or revenue line did you own? What were the numbers?
* Walk me through your growth model. Which stage is the constraint?
* How did you forecast, and how accurate were you?
* Tell me about a pricing change you owned. What happened to churn and to NRR?
* How do you work with sales when the roadmap and the pipeline disagree?
* Tell me about a build-vs-buy decision at meaningful cost. How did you decide?
* You need to cut 25% of costs and keep growth. What do you do?
* How do you report product performance to a board?
* What would you look at first in our numbers to judge product health?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you commit to, and did you hit it?"
* "What was the second-order effect you did not foresee?"
