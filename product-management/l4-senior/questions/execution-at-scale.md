# Execution at Scale

* Tell me about the largest programme you have run. How many people, how long?
    * What was the first milestone that delivered value on its own?
* How do you manage a dependency on a team that does not report to you and has other priorities?
* Describe a migration you led. What was the rollout plan and the rollback plan?
* What goes in your pre-mortem, and what has one actually caught for you?
* Something is six weeks late and the executive team finds out from someone else. What went wrong in your process?
* How do you decide to invest in platform work that produces no user-visible value this year?
* Tell me about a compliance or privacy constraint that reshaped a product decision.
* You are running three squads. How do you keep quality consistent across them?
* How do you tell an executive that a committed launch will not happen?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What specifically did you do, personally, in that programme?"
* "What was the earliest signal that it was going wrong, and did you act on it?"
