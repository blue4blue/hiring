# Product Strategy

* What is the strategy of the product you own today? Say it in three sentences.
    * What does that strategy say you will *not* do?
    * Who wrote it, and do you agree with it?
* Tell me about a strategy you changed. What was the trigger?
* How does your product make money, and what is the biggest lever on that number?
* Who are your real competitors, including the non-obvious ones?
    * What do they do better than you?
* If you had to double [our core metric] in 12 months, what would your plan be?
    * What would you stop doing to fund it?
* What is a bet you made that had no data behind it? How did you justify it?
* How do you tell the difference between a strategy and a wish list?
* What is the strongest argument against the strategy you just described?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What is the diagnosis — what is actually in the way?"
* "What has to be true first, and how would you know?"
* "What would you have done differently with half the budget?"
