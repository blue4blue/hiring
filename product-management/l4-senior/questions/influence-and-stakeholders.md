# Influence and Stakeholders

* Tell me about a time you changed the mind of someone more senior than you.
    * What did you prepare beforehand?
* Describe a conflict between two teams that you had to resolve.
* How do you structure a decision memo for an executive audience?
* Sales has promised a customer a feature that is not on your roadmap. Walk me through what you do.
* Tell me about a relationship you had to repair.
* How do you handle a stakeholder who keeps reopening a settled decision?
* How do you mentor a junior PM? Give me a specific example of feedback you gave.
* When have you escalated, and when have you deliberately not escalated?
* How do you say no to your own CEO?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was in it for them? How did you frame it in their terms?"
* "How did the relationship look six months later?"
