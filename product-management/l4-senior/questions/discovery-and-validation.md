# Discovery and Validation

* How do you run discovery for a whole area rather than a single feature?
* Tell me about a time the quantitative and qualitative evidence disagreed and the stakes were high.
* How do you test willingness to pay?
* You have a strategic bet that cannot be A/B tested. How do you de-risk it?
* Walk me through a churn analysis you ran. What did you find, and what did you do?
* How do you get research signal from enterprise customers who will not do usability tests?
* Tell me about a bet you made that was wrong. When did you know, and how long did it take to stop?
* How do you keep a research repository from becoming a graveyard?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the pre-registered hypothesis, and did the result actually match it?"
* "Which of those insights changed a roadmap item?"
