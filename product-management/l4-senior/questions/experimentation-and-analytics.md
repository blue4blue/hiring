# Experimentation and Analytics

* How many experiments does your team run per quarter, and what fraction win?
    * What do you do with the losers?
* How do you measure long-term impact when your tests run for two weeks?
* Your marketplace test shows a big win for the treated side. Why might that be an illusion?
* Explain how you would measure the impact of a change you cannot randomise — say, a pricing change rolled out by country.
* A metric is gameable and a team is gaming it. What do you do?
* How do you decide whether a metric is sensitive enough to be a team's target?
* Walk me through a time you disagreed with a data scientist's conclusion.
* How do you forecast the impact of a feature before you build it, and how often are you right?
* What is your event instrumentation governance? Who can add an event and how is it reviewed?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you pre-register, and what did you find afterwards?"
* "What is the honest error bar on that forecast?"
