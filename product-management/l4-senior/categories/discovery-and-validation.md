# Discovery and Validation

- Running discovery for an area, not a feature
- Portfolio of bets: cheap tests on many, deep investment on few
- Quantitative discovery: funnel mining, cohort forensics, churn analysis
- Combining sales/support/CS signal with direct research
- Market and competitor research that changes a decision
- Willingness-to-pay research and pricing tests
- Concierge and manual-first approaches for hard-to-test ideas
- Building a research repository the team actually reuses
- Discovery with regulated, enterprise or low-volume users
- Deciding under irreducible uncertainty and writing down the bet
