# Product Strategy

- Strategy as a diagnosis, a guiding policy and coherent action — not a list of features
- Where to play / how to win for a product area
- Positioning and differentiation; what you are deliberately bad at
- Market structure: substitutes, switching costs, distribution, network effects
- Business model literacy: unit economics, CAC/LTV, margin, payback
- Building a north star metric and connecting it to revenue
- Sequencing: what has to be true first
- Bets vs. maintenance vs. platform investment
- Writing a strategy document people can act on
- Knowing when the strategy is wrong and saying so
