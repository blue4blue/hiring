# Execution at Scale

- Running multiple squads or a large multi-quarter programme
- Breaking a big bet into independently valuable milestones
- Cross-team dependencies, interface contracts and integration risk
- Migrations, dual-writes, backfills and rollout plans
- Platform vs. feature trade-offs and paying for shared infrastructure
- Risk registers, pre-mortems and contingency planning
- Technical depth: data models, APIs, performance budgets, scalability limits
- Incident participation and post-incident product follow-through
- Legal, compliance, privacy and security as first-class constraints
- Knowing when to slow down and when to force a decision
