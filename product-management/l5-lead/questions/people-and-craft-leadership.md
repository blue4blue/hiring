# People and Craft Leadership

* How many PMs have you grown, and where are they now?
* Tell me about someone you coached from struggling to strong. What did you actually do?
* Tell me about someone you had to manage out or move. How did you handle it?
* What does a great spec look like to you? What do you send back?
* How do you review someone's strategy without rewriting it for them?
* How do you set a craft bar across several teams without becoming a bottleneck?
* Describe the feedback you have given most often in your career.
* How do you interview PMs? What is the one question you always ask, and why?
* When have you been wrong about someone's potential?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did they say when you gave that feedback?"
* "What changed in their work, specifically, and over what period?"
