# Strategy and Portfolio

* Describe the portfolio you own today. How do you allocate capacity across it?
    * What is over-funded? What is starved?
* What is the single constraint limiting your business right now? How do you know?
* Tell me about a product line you shut down or exited.
    * How did you decide? What did it cost, and who did it hurt?
* How do you decide how big a bet to make on something unproven?
* How do you distinguish a reversible decision from an irreversible one, and does that change your evidence bar?
* Walk me through a strategy document you wrote that changed what the company did.
* If our company had to pick exactly one thing to be great at, what should it be and why?
* What strategy did you hold for too long?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you deliberately starve to fund that?"
* "What would the strongest internal opponent of that strategy have said?"
* "How did you know it was working before the lagging metric moved?"
