# Business Impact

* Talk me through the P&L of the portfolio you owned. What were the biggest lines?
* What is the unit economics of your product, and which product decisions move them?
* Tell me about a pricing or packaging change you led.
    * What were the second-order effects you did not predict?
* You are asked to cut 20% of your budget. What goes, and how do you decide?
* How do you make the case for headcount you cannot directly attribute to revenue?
* When did you trade long-term product health for short-term revenue, or refuse to?
* How would you measure the impact of your portfolio if attribution is genuinely impossible?
* Explain your most important product decision last year to a CFO.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the payback period, and did it hold?"
* "What number did you commit to, and did you hit it?"
