# Operating Model and Execution

* How are your teams split today, and what would you change about the boundaries?
* Tell me about a process you removed. What happened?
* How do you set goals across a portfolio? What goes wrong with OKRs in practice?
* Two of your teams need the same platform work and neither wants to own it. What do you do?
* A team is busy, on time, and producing nothing that matters. Diagnose it.
* How do you keep quality consistent without reviewing everything yourself?
* How do you decide the decision rights — what comes to you and what does not?
* Describe your planning cadence. What is the one meeting you would not give up?
* Tell me about a programme that failed. What in the operating model allowed it to fail?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Where was the coordination cost, and what did you do to reduce it?"
* "What did you stop doing when you added that?"
