# Business Impact

- P&L literacy for the portfolio: revenue, cost, margin, payback
- Unit economics and where product decisions move them
- Pricing and packaging changes and their second-order effects
- Forecasting and being held to a number
- Build vs. buy vs. partner at the portfolio level
- Cost of goods sold: infrastructure, support, ops burden per feature
- Capital allocation: arguing for headcount and budget
- Measuring portfolio impact when attribution is genuinely hard
- Trading short-term revenue against long-term product health
- Explaining product decisions in the language of the CFO
