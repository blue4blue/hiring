# Strategy and Portfolio

- Owning strategy for a portfolio of areas or the hardest problem in the company
- Allocating capacity across bets, core work and platform investment
- Time horizons: this quarter, this year, the three-year shape
- Identifying the constraint that actually limits the business
- Making the case for entering — or exiting — a product line
- Strategy communication: the same idea for the team, the exec staff and the customer
- Managing a portfolio of risk: how many bets, how big, how reversible
- Reversible vs. irreversible decisions and how much evidence each deserves
- Killing a well-loved product and handling the fallout
- Setting the standard for what a good strategy document looks like
