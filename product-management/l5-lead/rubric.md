# Rubric — L5 Lead / Principal / Group Product Manager

At L5 the question is **leverage**. An L4 makes their area succeed. An L5 makes other
people and other teams succeed, and takes on the problems nobody else can hold. Two
shapes are valid: the people-leverage shape (Group PM) and the craft-leverage shape
(Principal PM). Assess against the shape you are actually hiring for.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategy & portfolio** | Strategy for one area only | Allocates across a portfolio; names the binding constraint | Redefines what the company should be doing and gets it funded |
| **People / craft leadership** | Does the work for others | Grows PMs; sets and holds a craft bar without bottlenecking | Has produced leaders who now operate independently at level |
| **Operating model** | Adds process to solve problems | Designs boundaries and cadence that reduce coordination cost | Diagnoses organisational dysfunction and fixes the cause |
| **Business impact** | Talks in feature metrics | Fluent in P&L, unit economics and trade-offs a CFO recognises | Has changed the economics of a business line through product |
| **Judgement under ambiguity** | Needs data to move | Decides with incomplete evidence and writes the bet down | Distinguishes reversible from irreversible and calibrates evidence to each |

## Must have

* Concrete evidence of growing other people — names, trajectories, and what they did.
* Ownership of a portfolio or a genuinely hard, cross-cutting problem.
* A strategy they wrote that changed what others did.
* Comfort with being accountable for a number.

## Nice to have

* Experience shutting something down, not just launching.
* Both people-management and individual-contributor phases in their career.
* Experience at our stage and one stage beyond it.

## Red flags

* Leverage described only as "I ran a bigger team".
* Every example is still individual delivery.
* Process added at every problem, never removed.
* Cannot name a person they made better.

## First 90 days if hired

Diagnose the portfolio and publish the allocation logic, establish the craft bar through
reviews, and identify the one organisational constraint to fix in the following quarter.
