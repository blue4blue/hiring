# Rubric — L1 Product Intern

An intern is hired on **potential and fundamentals**, not on outcomes. Do not expect
shipped products, owned metrics, or strategy. Expect curiosity, clarity and coachability.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product sense** | Describes features, cannot name a user or a problem | Names a real user and the problem they have; can critique a familiar product | Spontaneously reasons about trade-offs and who loses from a change |
| **Execution basics** | Confuses the roles; no idea what a backlog is | Understands stories, sprints and acceptance criteria; writes a clear ticket | Anticipates edge cases and asks clarifying questions unprompted |
| **Data and metrics** | Reads any number as good news | Defines conversion/retention correctly; asks for the denominator | Proposes a sensible way to check whether a change caused a result |
| **Communication** | Rambling, unstructured, no summary | Clear, structured, concise; asks when unsure | Adapts the explanation to the audience without being asked |
| **Coachability** | Defensive about feedback | Takes feedback and shows a change | Actively seeks feedback and reports what they did with it |

## Must have

* Clear written and spoken structure.
* Genuine curiosity about users — asks "why do they do that?" without prompting.
* Comfortable saying "I don't know" and then reasoning out loud.
* Basic numeracy: can talk about percentages and ratios without confusion.

## Nice to have

* Any hands-on artefact — a side project, a university project, a teardown, a Figma file, a SQL query.
* Basic SQL or spreadsheet modelling.
* Experience in a user-facing job of any kind (support, retail, teaching). It shows.

## Red flags

* Cannot describe a single product they like and why.
* Treats the interview as a test to be passed rather than a problem to explore.
* Blames others for every past setback.
* Confidently invents an answer rather than admitting a gap.

## First 90 days if hired

Shadow a PM, own one small feature slice end to end, run at least three user
interviews, and present one written recommendation to the team.
