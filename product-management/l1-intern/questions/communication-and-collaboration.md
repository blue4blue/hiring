# Communication and Collaboration

* Summarise a project you worked on in three sentences, for someone who knows nothing about it.
* Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate. What happened?
* You are in a meeting and you do not understand a term everyone else is using. What do you do?
* How would you explain a technical delay to a non-technical stakeholder?
* Tell me about feedback you received that stung. What did you do with it?
* You are behind on your task and the deadline is tomorrow. When do you say something, and to whom?
* What is the difference between what a PM does and what a designer does?
* You take notes in a user interview. What do you write down, and what do you leave out?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Show me — write the first two lines of that message."
* "What did the other person say back?"
