# Product Sense

* Pick a product you use every day. What problem does it solve, and for whom?
    * Who is *not* the user of that product?
    * How do you think it makes money?
* What is one thing you would change about it?
    * Who would be worse off if you made that change?
    * How would you know whether the change worked?
* What is the difference between a problem statement and a solution?
    * Turn "we need a dark mode" into a problem statement.
* Tell me about a product you stopped using. What made you stop?
* Here is a screenshot of a signup flow. What do you notice?
    * Where would you expect people to drop off, and why?
* If we wanted to help first-time users understand our app faster, what would you look at first?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who told you that, or did you assume it?" — separates observation from guesswork.
* "What would have to be true for that idea to be a bad one?"
