# Execution Basics

* What is a backlog, and who decides the order of it?
* Write a user story for "the user can reset their password".
    * What are the acceptance criteria?
    * What edge cases would you list?
* What is the difference between Scrum and Kanban?
    * Which one would you pick for a team fixing production bugs all day?
* Why do teams estimate at all, if the estimates are usually wrong?
* A developer tells you a ticket is unclear. What do you do?
* Mid-sprint, a stakeholder asks for a new "quick" feature. What do you do?
* What is a feature flag, and why would you use one?
* You have five bugs and two days. How do you decide the order?
    * What information would you go find first?
