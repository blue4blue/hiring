# Rubric — L2 Associate Product Manager

An APM owns a feature or a small surface. They are assessed on **reliable execution and
sound fundamentals**, with judgement still developing. Expect them to escalate rather
than to decide alone on anything strategic — that is correct behaviour at this level.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product sense** | Talks in features; no user in the story | Frames problems before solutions; names trade-offs | Sizes the opportunity and argues for not building something |
| **Discovery** | Has never spoken to a user directly | Runs interviews, avoids leading questions, synthesises to themes | Designs a cheap test for the riskiest assumption without prompting |
| **Execution** | Passive ticket-mover; surprised by slips | Owns a backlog, writes clear specs, flags risk early | Slices work to ship value in week one; unblocks dependencies themselves |
| **Metrics** | Reports numbers with no interpretation | Picks a primary metric and a guardrail; reads a test correctly | Diagnoses a metric move by segment and finds the real cause |
| **Communication** | Needs prompting to structure an update | Written updates are clear and decision-oriented | Adjusts the message for engineering, design and leadership |

## Must have

* Has personally talked to users and can tell you what they learned.
* Owned at least one feature end to end, including the launch.
* Can name the metric their work moved and how they knew.
* Writes clearly under pressure.

## Nice to have

* Working SQL.
* Experience with A/B testing in practice, not just theory.
* Has killed or de-scoped something and can explain why.

## Red flags

* Describes the role entirely as ceremonies, tickets and status meetings.
* No metric attached to any shipped work.
* Every plan slipped because of "engineering" or "stakeholders".
* Cannot describe a single disagreement they were part of.

## First 90 days if hired

Own one surface, ship at least two increments, run continuous user contact, and present
one data-backed recommendation that changes the roadmap.
