# Execution and Delivery

- Owning a backlog: grooming, splitting, sequencing
- Writing specs and acceptance criteria engineers do not have to guess at
- Slicing work vertically so every increment is shippable
- Dependencies: identifying them early and tracking them
- Estimation, buffers, and communicating uncertainty honestly
- Release process: staging, canary, feature flags, rollback
- QA, edge cases, and the non-happy path
- Incident awareness: what a PM does when production breaks
- Launch checklist: docs, support, analytics, comms
- Post-launch: monitoring, iteration, and knowing when to stop
