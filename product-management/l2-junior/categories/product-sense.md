# Product Sense

- Users, jobs to be done, and the difference between a need and a request
- Personas and segmentation that actually change decisions
- User journeys, funnels and the moments that matter
- Value proposition: what the user gets, what they give up
- Opportunity sizing at a rough order of magnitude
- Competitive awareness: alternatives, including "do nothing" and "spreadsheet"
- Product critique: hierarchy, friction, defaults, empty states, error states
- Trade-offs: scope vs. quality vs. time, and who absorbs each one
- Knowing when a problem is not worth solving
