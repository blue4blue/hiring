# Product Sense

* Take our product. Who is the user, and what job are they hiring it to do?
    * Who is the user we serve worst today?
* Design a feature for [a specific segment we care about]. Walk me through your thinking.
    * What did you decide *not* to build, and why?
    * What is the smallest version that still tests the idea?
* Tell me about a feature you shipped that did not work.
    * How did you find out? How long did it take you to find out?
    * What did you do with it — iterate, kill, or leave it?
* Pick a product you admire and one design decision in it you think is wrong.
* We have a request from our biggest customer for a feature only they will use. How do you handle it?
* How would you size the opportunity for [a plausible new feature]? Rough numbers are fine.
* When is the right answer "we should not build this"?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What evidence did you have, versus what you assumed?"
* "Who inside the company disagreed with you?"
