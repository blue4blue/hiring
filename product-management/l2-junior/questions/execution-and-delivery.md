# Execution and Delivery

* Walk me through how a feature goes from idea to live on your current team.
    * Where does it usually get stuck?
* Show me a spec you wrote. What would you change about it now?
* Engineering says the feature will take 8 weeks; the business needs it in 4. What do you do?
    * What do you cut first, and how do you decide?
* How do you slice a large feature so you can ship something in week one?
* A dependency on another team slips. When and how do you escalate?
* You are two days from launch and QA finds a serious bug. Walk me through the next hour.
* What is on your launch checklist?
* How do you know when a feature is "done"?
* Tell me about a project that ran late. What was the real cause?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you personally do, as opposed to the team?"
* "What would you do differently if the same thing happened next month?"
