# Discovery and User Research

* How many users have you personally talked to in the last three months?
    * What did you learn that surprised you?
* Write me the first three questions of an interview guide for [a problem area].
    * Which of those is leading, and how would you fix it?
* A user says "I would definitely pay for that." How much do you trust it?
* How do you decide whether to run interviews, a survey, or a usability test?
* What is your riskiest assumption on your current project, and how would you test it in a week?
* Tell me about research that changed your mind about a plan.
    * What would it have cost if you had not done it?
* You have five interview transcripts. How do you turn them into a decision?
* Support says "everyone is complaining about X." What do you do next?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Did you talk to people who did *not* have the problem?"
* "How did you separate what they said from what they did?"
