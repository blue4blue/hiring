# Metrics and Analytics

* What was the primary metric for the last feature you shipped? Why that one?
    * What was your guardrail metric?
* Retention is flat at 20% after week four. Is that good or bad?
* Sign-ups are up 30% and activation is down 10%. What happened?
* How would you instrument a new checkout flow? What events would you define?
* Explain statistical significance to an engineer who does not care about statistics.
* We ran an A/B test for three days and got a 5% lift, p = 0.04. Would you ship it?
    * What would you check first?
* How do you set a target for a metric you have never measured before?
* Given users and orders tables, write the SQL for weekly retention of the January cohort.
* A metric drops 40% overnight. Walk me through your diagnosis.
