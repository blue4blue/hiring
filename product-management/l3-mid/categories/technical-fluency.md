# Technical Fluency

- Client / server / database / API: the shape of the system you own
- REST vs. GraphQL vs. gRPC at the level of trade-offs, not syntax
- Sync vs. async, queues, and eventual consistency
- Latency, caching, and why "just add a field" is sometimes a month
- Data models and migrations
- Third-party integrations, rate limits and failure modes
- Build vs. buy vs. integrate
- Tech debt: recognising it, funding it, and arguing for it
- Security and privacy basics: PII, GDPR-style consent, auth vs. authz
- Reading a system diagram and asking the right question about it
- Talking to engineers about trade-offs without pretending to be one
