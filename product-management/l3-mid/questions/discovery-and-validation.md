# Discovery and Validation

* Describe your discovery cadence. What happens in a normal week?
* What are the three biggest assumptions behind your current roadmap?
    * Which one would hurt most if it were wrong? How would you test it this week?
* Tell me about the cheapest experiment you have run that saved the most work.
* When have you had qualitative and quantitative data pointing in opposite directions? What did you do?
* How do you recruit users to talk to when you have no research team?
* You have three weeks and a hypothesis that people will pay for X. What do you do?
* How do you avoid building for the loudest customer?
* Tell me about research you ran that everyone ignored. Why did that happen?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the falsification criterion — what result would have stopped the project?"
* "How many people, over what period, and how did you pick them?"
