# Product Sense and Judgement

* Tell me about the hardest product decision you made in the last year.
    * What were the options? What did you give up?
    * Who was unhappy with the decision, and how did you handle that?
* Our [core metric] has been flat for two quarters. Where do you start?
* Design [a plausible feature for our product]. Take me through problem, users, solution, and how you would validate it.
    * What is the riskiest part of that plan?
    * What would the v0.1 be if you had two weeks?
* Tell me about something you killed. What did it cost to kill it, and what did you learn?
* A customer worth 15% of revenue demands a feature that hurts the other 85%. What do you do?
* When was the last time you refused to build something? Why?
* Critique our onboarding. What are the three biggest problems, ranked?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the counter-argument, stated as well as its strongest advocate would state it?"
* "What data would have changed your decision?"
* "What did you actually own here, versus your manager?"
