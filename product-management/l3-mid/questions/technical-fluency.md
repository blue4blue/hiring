# Technical Fluency

* Draw the architecture of the product you last worked on, at whatever level you understand it.
    * Where were the bottlenecks?
* An engineer says "that field is not in our data model, it will take three weeks." How do you respond?
* What is the difference between a synchronous call and a queue, and when does that difference reach the user?
* Your feature depends on a third-party API that is down 1% of the time. What do you do about it?
* How do you decide between building something and buying it?
* How have you argued for tech debt work with a business stakeholder?
* What personal data does your product collect, and what happens if a user asks you to delete it?
* An engineer proposes a rewrite. How do you evaluate that?
* Something in production is broken and users are affected. What is your role in the next 30 minutes?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you not understand, and how did you get to the point where you could decide?"
* "What was the cheapest option you rejected, and why?"
