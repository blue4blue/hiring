# Metrics and Experimentation

* Draw the metric tree for your product. Where does your team sit on it?
* What was the OEC of your last experiment, and why that metric?
* Your test shows +3% on the primary metric, p = 0.03, and −2% on a guardrail, p = 0.12. Ship or not?
* How do you compute the sample size you need before running a test?
    * What do you do when the answer is "six months of traffic"?
* Why is peeking at results a problem, and what do you do instead?
* We tested 15 metrics and one is significant. What do you conclude?
* When would you refuse to run an A/B test?
* A feature won the A/B test and then the metric decayed after three months. What happened?
* How do you measure a change that only affects 2% of users but is strategically important?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the minimum detectable effect, and who agreed it was worth detecting?"
* "Which segments did you look at, and did you decide that before or after seeing the data?"
