# Prioritisation and Roadmap

* Walk me through how your current roadmap was built.
    * Which parts are commitments and which are forecasts?
* What prioritisation framework do you use, and where does it break down?
* You have four things and capacity for two. Talk me through the choice.
    * What is the opportunity cost of the two you picked?
* How much of your team's capacity goes to tech debt and KTLO? How did you land on that number?
* A VP adds an item to your roadmap in a meeting. What happens next?
* You have to move a committed date by six weeks. Who do you tell, in what order, and what do you say?
* How do you decide between a big bet and five small improvements?
* What is on your roadmap right now that you privately think is a mistake?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you take *off* the roadmap to make room?"
* "How did you communicate that to the people who cared about it?"
