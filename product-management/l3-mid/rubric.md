# Rubric — L3 Product Manager

A PM owns a product area with its own metric. They are assessed on **judgement, not
just delivery**: the right thing shipped, for stated reasons, with evidence.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product sense** | Solutions arrive before problems | Frames problems, weighs options, states the trade-off | Argues convincingly against work others wanted, with evidence |
| **Discovery** | Research is a phase, if it happens | Weekly user contact; tests the riskiest assumption cheaply | Designs falsifiable tests and has killed an idea on the result |
| **Prioritisation** | Prioritises by who asked loudest | Has a defensible method and can name the opportunity cost | Reshapes the roadmap around an outcome and defends it upward |
| **Metrics & experimentation** | Ships and hopes; reads p-values superstitiously | Sets OEC and guardrails; designs and reads tests correctly | Catches the subtle failure mode — peeking, interference, decay |
| **Technical fluency** | Cannot describe how their product works | Discusses trade-offs credibly; earns engineering's respect | Shapes technical decisions by reframing the product constraint |
| **Communication** | Updates are activity logs | Writes decision-oriented docs; disagrees productively | Aligns skeptical stakeholders without escalation |

## Must have

* Owned a metric, moved it, and can explain the causal story.
* At least one decision they made that turned out wrong, told without spin.
* Direct, regular contact with users.
* Credibility with engineers — evidenced by how they describe technical trade-offs.

## Nice to have

* Experience in our domain or with a comparable business model.
* Hands-on SQL or analytics tooling.
* Has run a genuine discovery programme, not just delivery.

## Red flags

* Every story is about delivery velocity, never about outcome.
* Cannot name what they chose not to do.
* Describes stakeholders as obstacles rather than as inputs.
* Attributes all learning to a framework and none to a user.

## First 90 days if hired

Own the area's metric, publish a written point of view on the area within six weeks,
run continuous discovery, and land one change with a measured result.
