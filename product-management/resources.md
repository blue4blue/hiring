# Resources

Reading and reference material for product management candidates and for the
interviewers assessing them. Not a required reading list — a starting point.

## Books

### Craft and discovery

* Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love, by Marty Cagan
* Empowered: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products, by Marty Cagan and Chris Jones
* Continuous Discovery Habits, by Teresa Torres
* The Mom Test, by Rob Fitzpatrick
* Escaping the Build Trap, by Melissa Perri

### Strategy

* Good Strategy / Bad Strategy, by Richard Rumelt
* Playing to Win, by A.G. Lafley and Roger Martin
* Competing Against Luck, by Clayton Christensen (Jobs to be Done)
* The Innovator's Dilemma, by Clayton Christensen

### Metrics, experimentation and growth

* Lean Analytics, by Alistair Croll and Benjamin Yoskovitz
* Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments, by Kohavi, Tang and Xu
* Hacking Growth, by Sean Ellis and Morgan Brown

### Leadership and organisation

* An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management, by Will Larson
* The Making of a Manager, by Julie Zhuo
* High Output Management, by Andrew Grove
* Team Topologies, by Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais
* Measure What Matters, by John Doerr

### Adjacent but essential

* Thinking, Fast and Slow, by Daniel Kahneman
* Designing Data-Intensive Applications, by Martin Kleppmann (for technical fluency)
* Don't Make Me Think, by Steve Krug
