# Rubric — L1 Product Ops Intern

Assessed on **organisation, clarity and reliability**. Expect no operating-model design;
expect someone who makes things tidier and follows through.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Process understanding** | Cannot describe how work flows | Knows the rituals and what each is for | Spots where a process creates waste and says why |
| **Tooling** | No hands-on tool experience | Competent in at least one tracker and one docs tool | Automates something without being asked |
| **Reporting** | Lists activity | Summarises accurately with the exception highlighted | Reports the decision needed, not just the status |
| **Communication** | Vague, long, or silent | Clear, short, timely; escalates proportionately | Turns a stalled thread into a decision |
| **Reliability** | Misses commitments quietly | Does what they said, flags early when they cannot | Others start relying on them within weeks |

## Must have

* Evidence of organising something real — a project, a club, an event, a team's files.
* Clear written communication.
* Basic spreadsheet competence.
* Follows through without reminders.

## Nice to have

* Any tracker or documentation tool experience.
* Basic automation (Zapier, formulas, scripts).
* Customer-facing experience.

## Red flags

* Describes process as an end in itself.
* Cannot give an example of following up on something.
* Vague about what they personally did in a team effort.

## First 90 days if hired

Own one recurring report, tidy one team's tracker and documentation, and automate one
manual step.
