# Communication and Coordination

- Writing a clear, short update people actually read
- Running a meeting to a decision, not to the end of the hour
- Taking notes that capture decisions and owners
- Chasing without nagging
- Coordinating across teams and time zones
- Asking clarifying questions before starting work
- Escalating early and proportionately
- Handling a stakeholder who does not respond
- Keeping a source of truth and pointing people to it
- Being reliable: doing what you said, when you said
