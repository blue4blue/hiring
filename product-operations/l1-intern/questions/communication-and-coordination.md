# Communication and Coordination

* Write the first three lines of an update about a project that has slipped.
* You are running a meeting and it is going nowhere. What do you do?
* How do you follow up with someone who has ignored you twice?
* You are coordinating three teams in different time zones. How do you run it?
* Tell me about a time you kept something on track that was not your job.
* What do you do when two people give you conflicting instructions?
* How do you decide whether something needs a meeting, a doc or a message?
* Tell me about a commitment you missed. What did you do?
