# Process Basics

* Describe how work gets from an idea to a customer in a team you have seen.
* What is the difference between a backlog and a roadmap?
* What is a retro for, and what makes a bad one?
* Why do teams have a definition of done?
* A team's standup takes 40 minutes. What would you look at?
* What has to happen before a feature can launch, beyond the code being finished?
* Someone asks the product team for something in a Slack DM. What is wrong with that, and what would you do?
* Give me an example of a process that created more work than it saved.

## Follow-ups to test depth
