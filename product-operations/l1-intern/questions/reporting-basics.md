# Reporting Basics

* What goes in a weekly status report? What do you leave out?
* Show me how you would summarise 30 tickets into three lines.
* What is cycle time, and why would a team care?
* A chart shows velocity rising. Is that good?
* How do you report a project that is behind without either hiding it or causing panic?
* What would you automate in your reporting, and what must stay manual?
* You find two dashboards with different numbers for the same thing. What do you do?
* How do you check a number before publishing it?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What is the action you want the reader to take?"
* "What would you cut if you had two sentences?"
