# Documentation and Enablement

- Templates: specs, briefs, launch plans, decision records
- A knowledge base people can navigate without asking
- Onboarding material for new product team members
- Launch enablement: what support, sales and success need and when
- Release notes and internal comms
- Training people on a new process or tool
- Capturing decisions so they are not relitigated
- Keeping documentation alive rather than letting it rot
- Feedback loops: how you learn the docs are wrong
- Writing for people who will skim
