# Documentation and Enablement

* What templates have you created? Did people use them?
* How do you structure a knowledge base so people find things?
* Design an onboarding plan for a new PM's first two weeks.
* What do support and sales need before a launch, and when do they need it?
* How do you train a team on a new process?
* How do you stop the same decision from being relitigated every quarter?
* How do you keep documentation from rotting?
* Tell me about documentation you wrote that nobody read. Why?
