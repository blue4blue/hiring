# Data and Reporting

* Which delivery metrics do you track, and what do they tell you?
* Cycle time doubled last month. Walk me through your investigation.
* How do you report roadmap progress across five teams in one page?
* Show me a dashboard you built. Who used it?
* How do you pull data from a tracker — API, export, tool-native?
* What is the difference between an activity metric and an outcome metric here?
* What have you automated in reporting, and what time did it save?
* You publish a number and someone finds it is wrong. What do you do?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What decision did that report drive?"
* "What did you stop reporting, and did anyone notice?"
