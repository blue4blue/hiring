# Rubric — L2 Product Operations Associate

Runs one process or one part of the stack reliably. Assessed on **execution, hygiene and
follow-through**; design of the operating model is not expected yet.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cadence** | Attends rituals | Runs rituals to decisions; manages intake | Redesigns a ritual and shows the improvement |
| **Tooling** | Basic user | Administers tools; builds workflows and automations | Simplifies the stack or retires a tool |
| **Reporting** | Compiles status | Builds automated reports; explains anomalies | Reports drive decisions, not just awareness |
| **Enablement** | Writes docs nobody reads | Templates and docs people actually use | Onboarding time measurably shortens |
| **Follow-through** | Things drop | Nothing assigned to them is forgotten | Others rely on them to close loops across teams |

## Must have

* Hands-on administration of at least one core tool.
* Has run recurring rituals themselves.
* Has automated something and can say what it saved.
* Precise, short written communication.

## Nice to have

* SQL or tool-native query skills.
* Feedback tooling and taxonomy experience.
* Customer-facing background.

## Red flags

* Adds process at every problem, removes none.
* Cannot name a measurable improvement.
* Describes the role purely as running meetings.
* Documentation with no evidence anyone used it.

## First 90 days if hired

Own the cadence for one product group, clean up the tracker and taxonomy, automate one
recurring report, and cut one meeting.
