# Rubric — L3 Product Operations Manager

Owns the operating cadence, stack and insight pipeline for a product group. Assessed on
**designing process that measurably helps**, not on administering it.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cadence** | Runs the existing rituals | Designs cadence for a group; kills what does not earn its place | Measurably reduced planning time or coordination cost |
| **Tooling & systems** | Administers tools | Selects, migrates and consolidates with a cost argument | Retired overlap and improved adoption with evidence |
| **Insight** | Collates feedback | Runs a weighted, deduped pipeline that reaches decisions | Insight from their pipeline changed the roadmap |
| **Launch readiness** | Maintains a checklist | Owns tiering, go/no-go and enablement across functions | Stopped or rescued a launch on defensible grounds |
| **Change management** | Announces changes | Gets people to adopt new ways of working | Changed behaviour in a team that resisted |

## Must have

* A process they removed or simplified, with a measured result.
* Ownership of a real tool stack including a migration or selection.
* Launch coordination across at least three functions.
* Evidence of adoption, not just rollout.

## Nice to have

* SQL or analytics fluency.
* Experience in a regulated or enterprise launch context.
* Programme management certification or equivalent rigour.

## Red flags

* Only ever added process.
* No measurement of any process outcome.
* Treats adoption as someone else's problem.
* Describes the role as being the PM's assistant.

## First 90 days if hired

Map the current operating model, cut the two lowest-value rituals, fix intake, and put
one measured improvement in place (planning duration, cycle time or decision latency).
