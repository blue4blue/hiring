# Launch Readiness and Enablement

* What is on your launch checklist? Which item do people most often skip?
* How do you tier launches, and what changes between tiers?
* Who makes the go/no-go call in your process, and on what criteria?
* Tell me about a launch you stopped. What was the fallout?
* How do you enable support and sales, and how far in advance?
* Describe a beta programme you ran. How did you pick participants?
* A launch causes an incident. What is your role in the first hour?
* How do you run a post-launch review that is honest?
* How do you communicate a deprecation to customers?
