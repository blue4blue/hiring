# Tooling and Systems

* Describe the product stack you own. What is it costing, and is it earning that?
* Walk me through a tool selection you ran, from requirements to decision.
* Tell me about a migration you executed. What went wrong?
* How do you measure tool adoption, and what did you do when it was low?
* Two tools disagree because a sync silently failed. How do you detect that in future?
* When would you build something internally instead of buying?
* How do you handle a security or procurement review?
* What tool have you retired, and how did you handle the objections?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the switching cost you underestimated?"
* "What percentage of the team actually used it after six months?"
