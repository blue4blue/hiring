# Operating Cadence

* Describe the cadence you designed or ran. What happens weekly, monthly, quarterly?
* How long does your quarterly planning take, and what would you cut from it?
* How do you keep goals to a number that creates focus?
* A team keeps changing committed roadmap items mid-quarter. What do you do?
* How are decision rights defined in your org? What is unclear?
* Two teams have a dependency neither is tracking. How do you find out and what do you do?
* A group is busy, on time, and producing nothing that matters. Diagnose it.
* What ritual did you kill? What happened?
* How do you know your cadence is working?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did the cycle time or planning duration look like before and after?"
* "Who resisted, and how did you get them across?"
