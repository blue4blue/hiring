# Insights and Voice of Customer

* Describe the feedback pipeline you built. What sources feed it?
* How do you design a taxonomy that survives two years of product change?
* How do you weight feedback — by volume, revenue, segment, or something else?
* How do you stop the loudest customer from dominating the roadmap?
* How do you make insight available at the moment a PM is deciding, rather than in a monthly report?
* How do you close the loop with customers whose feedback shipped?
* Tell me about an insight theme that changed the roadmap.
* What feedback do you deliberately ignore?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "How many items a month, and how much of it is duplicated?"
* "Which roadmap item exists because of that pipeline?"
