# Launch Readiness and Enablement

- Launch tiers: what a tier-1 launch requires versus a tier-3
- Launch checklists that cover legal, support, sales, comms, analytics and rollback
- Go/no-go criteria and who makes the call
- Cross-functional launch calendars and conflict management
- Enablement: training, documentation and FAQs for internal teams
- Beta and early-access programme management
- Rollout mechanics: flags, staged release, kill switches
- Post-launch review and the metrics that trigger action
- Incident coordination when a launch goes wrong
- Sunsetting and deprecation communications
