# Operating Cadence

- Designing the cadence for a product group: what happens weekly, monthly, quarterly
- Quarterly planning: inputs, sequence, timeboxes, outputs
- Goal setting that produces focus rather than a spreadsheet exercise
- Roadmap governance: commitment discipline and change control
- Decision rights and escalation paths that people actually use
- Intake and triage at group scale
- Dependency tracking across teams
- Risk registers and status that reflects reality
- Reducing coordination cost: fewer meetings, better artefacts
- Diagnosing why a group is busy but not delivering outcomes
