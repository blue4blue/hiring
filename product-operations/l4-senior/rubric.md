# Rubric — L4 Senior Product Operations Manager

Owns cross-team processes and runs the programmes nobody else can hold together.
Assessed on **process design, programme delivery and change management**.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Process design** | Standardises everything | Designs for many teams; standardises selectively; measures the model | Removed significant coordination cost with evidence |
| **Systems** | Administers a stack | Owns architecture, integrations, vendors and cost | Consolidated a sprawling stack and proved the saving |
| **Insight programmes** | Runs a feedback report | Runs a weighted programme that reaches decisions | Insight programme changed strategy, not just features |
| **Programme management** | Tracks status | Runs multi-function programmes with honest status and real risk management | Rescued a programme heading for failure |
| **Change management** | Announces and hopes | Pilots, adapts and drives adoption | Changed behaviour in a resistant org and it stuck |

## Must have

* A measured improvement to an operating model.
* A multi-function programme they personally ran.
* A tool or vendor decision with a cost argument.
* At least one failure they diagnosed honestly.

## Nice to have

* Regulated or multi-geography launch experience.
* Analytics fluency.
* Experience in both scale-up and larger-company environments.

## Red flags

* Status was always green until it was not.
* No process ever removed.
* Programme stories with no numbers.
* Change described purely as communication.

## First 90 days if hired

Map the operating model end to end, publish the diagnosis with measures, pilot one
change, and take ownership of the highest-risk cross-functional programme.
