# Insight Programmes

- Running voice-of-customer as a programme, not a report
- Combining support, sales, CS, research, community and analytics signal
- Segment- and revenue-weighted prioritisation of feedback
- Competitive and market intelligence as a standing capability
- Research ops: recruiting, incentives, consent, repository, reuse
- Insight quality: avoiding confirmation bias in what gets surfaced
- Making insight actionable at the decision moment
- Metrics for the insight programme itself
- Executive-level insight reporting
- Closing the loop with customers and with commercial teams
