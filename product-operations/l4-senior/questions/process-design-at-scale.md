# Process Design at Scale

* Describe a process you designed for many teams. How did you handle teams that needed to differ?
* How do you decide what to standardise and what to leave alone?
* Walk me through your planning process at scale. Where does bottom-up meet top-down?
* What do you measure about the operating model itself?
* Tell me about a process change you piloted. What did the pilot change about the rollout?
* How do you stop process from accumulating?
* Tell me about a process change that failed. Why?
* How do you handle a senior leader who wants a process that you think is wrong?
