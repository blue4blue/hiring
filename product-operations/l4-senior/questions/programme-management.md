# Cross-functional Programme Management

* Tell me about the largest programme you have run: functions, people, duration.
* How did you break it into milestones with independent value?
* How do you map the critical path, and what did you do when it moved?
* What is in your risk register, and what has a pre-mortem actually caught?
* How do you make status honest when everyone is incentivised to be green?
* Describe a steering committee you ran. Did it decide anything?
* Scope grew 40% mid-programme. What did you do?
* Tell me about a programme that failed. What was the real cause?
* How do you review benefits after the programme closes?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "When did you first know it would slip, and who did you tell?"
* "What decision did the steering group make that you disagreed with?"
