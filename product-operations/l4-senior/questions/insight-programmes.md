# Insight Programmes

* Describe the voice-of-customer programme you ran end to end.
* How did you weight signal across segments and revenue?
* How did you keep the programme from surfacing only what leadership wanted to hear?
* How did you make insight reach a PM at the moment of decision?
* What research ops did you build — recruiting, consent, repository?
* How do you measure whether the insight programme is working?
* Tell me about an insight that changed strategy, not just a feature.
* How do you run competitive intelligence as a standing capability rather than an occasional deck?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Which roadmap decisions can you trace back to it?"
* "What did you stop collecting?"
