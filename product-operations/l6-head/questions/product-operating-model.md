# Product Operating Model

* Describe the product operating model you owned. What were its components?
* How did you hold empowerment and commitment together?
* What did you measure, and what did the numbers show over four quarters?
* How does an idea from an engineer reach the roadmap in your model?
* How did the model change as the company changed stage?
* Tell me about diagnosing a problem as strategy or people rather than process.
* What part of the model did you inherit and keep?
* If you looked at us, where would you expect the operating model to be weakest?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was predictability or decision latency before and after?"
