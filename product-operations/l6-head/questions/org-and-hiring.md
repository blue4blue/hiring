# Org and Hiring

* Describe the product ops team you built: size, shape, split of flavours.
* What ratio of product ops to product teams did you run, and why?
* How do you define levels for a discipline with no industry standard?
* Walk me through your hiring loop for a senior product ops role.
* How many have you hired? What did the misses have in common?
* Tell me about managing someone out.
* How do you keep the team small rather than growing it because you can?
* How do you partner with programme management or chief-of-staff functions?
* How do you give people a career when the ladder is short?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Where are those people now?"
* "What work did you deliberately give back to product teams?"
