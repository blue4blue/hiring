# Business and Stakeholders

* What budget did you own — team and stack?
* Make the commercial case for product ops to a CFO.
* Describe your relationship with the CPO and CTO. Where did you disagree?
* Tell me about an executive demand you refused because it would damage the system.
* How do you translate operating metrics into business outcomes?
* Tell me about communicating a major change to the whole organisation.
* How do you handle the operational fallout of a reorganisation?
* When have you used consultants, and how did that go?
* How do you stay the honest broker between teams and leadership?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What number did you tie the investment to?"
* "What did you concede, and was it right?"
