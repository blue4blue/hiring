# Planning and Governance

* Walk me through your planning cycle. How long does it take end to end?
* How do you set the investment mix across teams?
* How is roadmap commitment enforced, and what happens when it breaks?
* What does executive reporting look like in your org? What did you remove from it?
* How do legal, privacy and compliance checkpoints fit into the product process?
* Tell me about a governance gate you added. Did it slow things down?
* How do you maintain institutional memory of decisions?
* How do you review whether an investment delivered what was promised?
* Tell me about coordinating a crisis across the org.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What percentage of the year goes into planning?"
* "What did the benefit review actually find?"
