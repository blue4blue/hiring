# Planning and Governance

- Owning the planning cycle for a business line end to end
- Capacity, allocation and investment mix across teams
- Roadmap governance and commitment discipline at org level
- Portfolio risk, dependency and escalation management
- Decision records and institutional memory
- Executive reporting: what leadership sees and how often
- Launch governance and quality gates
- Compliance, legal and privacy checkpoints in the product process
- Incident and crisis coordination
- Benefit realisation: did the investment produce what was promised?
