# Product Operating Model

- Owning how product gets done across a business line
- Discovery, delivery and decision-making as one designed system
- Team topology and ownership in partnership with product and engineering leadership
- Planning architecture and the annual/quarterly/continuous split
- Goal system design and its honest failure modes
- Empowerment and accountability held together
- Operating metrics: predictability, cycle time, decision latency, planning cost
- Continuous improvement of the model itself
- Adapting the model as the company changes stage
- Knowing when the model is fine and the problem is people or strategy
