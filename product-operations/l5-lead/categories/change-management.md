# Change Management

- Diagnosing why people work the way they do before changing it
- Building a coalition before announcing a change
- Piloting, measuring and adapting rather than mandating
- Communicating the why in the terms each audience cares about
- Handling resistance: legitimate objections versus inertia
- Sequencing changes so the org can absorb them
- Making new behaviour easier than the old behaviour
- Sustaining a change after the attention moves on
- Knowing when to stop pushing a change that is not working
- Rebuilding trust after a failed change programme
