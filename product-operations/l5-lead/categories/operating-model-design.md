# Operating Model Design

- Designing how the whole product organisation works: discovery, delivery, decisions
- Team topology and ownership boundaries in partnership with product and engineering leaders
- Planning architecture: annual, quarterly, continuous — and what each actually decides
- Goal systems that create focus rather than reporting overhead
- Decision rights, escalation and the cost of unclear ownership
- Empowerment versus commitment: making both true at once
- Measuring the operating model and publishing the numbers
- Designing for the organisation you will be in 18 months
- Removing process at the same rate you add it
- Knowing when the problem is structure, not process
