# Tooling Strategy

- A stack strategy tied to the operating model, not to feature lists
- Consolidation versus best-of-breed and the honest trade-off
- Total cost of ownership including administration and people's time
- Data architecture across tools and the reporting layer above it
- Vendor strategy, negotiation leverage and exit planning
- Security, privacy and compliance posture of the stack
- Internal build decisions and their maintenance liability
- Change and migration programmes across the whole org
- Measuring value delivered per tool
- Saying no to tools that solve a process problem badly
