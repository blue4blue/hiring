# Mentoring and Influence

* How do you define what good looks like in a discipline with no standard definition?
* Who have you grown in product ops? Where are they now?
* How do you coach a PM or an EM on running their team without stepping on their manager?
* Tell me about influencing a decision made by product and engineering leadership.
* How do you present the operating model to an exec team in business terms?
* Tell me about saying no to a leadership request that would have damaged the system.
* How do you facilitate a hard conversation between two functions?
* How do you interview for a role nobody defines the same way?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you say, and what did they say back?"
* "What did it cost you, and was it worth it?"
