# Operating Model Design

* Describe the operating model you designed. What were the components?
* How did you balance team empowerment with commitments the business needed?
* How do you decide what planning happens annually, quarterly and continuously?
* What do you measure about the operating model, and what did those numbers show?
* Tell me about a time the problem was structure, not process. What did you do?
* How do you design for the organisation you will be in 18 months?
* What did you remove last year?
* You inherit an org where planning takes six weeks a quarter. What is your plan?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the planning duration or decision latency before and after?"
* "Who owned the model after you — did it survive?"
