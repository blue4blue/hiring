# Tooling Strategy

* What is your stack strategy — consolidation or best-of-breed? Defend it.
* How do you calculate total cost of ownership including people's time?
* Tell me about a vendor negotiation. What leverage did you have?
* How do you architect data across tools so reporting is possible at all?
* Tell me about a tool you refused to buy. What was the underlying problem?
* Describe the largest migration you have led across the org.
* How do you handle a leader who wants a tool because a peer company uses it?
* How do you measure value per tool?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did that cost in year one versus year three?"
* "What was the exit plan if the vendor failed you?"
