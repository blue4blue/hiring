# Change Management

* Tell me about the biggest behaviour change you have driven. How long did it take?
* How do you diagnose why people work the way they do before you change it?
* How do you build a coalition before announcing something?
* Tell me about resistance you encountered that turned out to be right.
* How do you make the new way easier than the old way?
* Tell me about a change that did not stick. What happened after the attention moved on?
* When did you stop pushing a change? How did you decide?
* How do you rebuild trust after a failed change programme?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you change about your plan after the pilot?"
* "How did you know the behaviour had actually changed, not just the tooling?"
