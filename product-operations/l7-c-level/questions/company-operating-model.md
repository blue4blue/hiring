# Company Operating Model

* Describe the company operating model you owned. What were its parts, and what did each decide?
* How did you align product, engineering, go-to-market and finance rhythms?
* Where was the biggest gap between strategy and execution, and what closed it?
* How does capital and headcount get allocated in your model?
* What operating metrics did the exec team run on? Which were misleading?
* How did the model change through growth, acquisition or contraction?
* What organisational drag did you remove, and what did it save?
* Tell me about a time the operating model could not fix the problem.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the measurable effect on the company, not the process?"
* "What did the CEO stop doing as a result?"
