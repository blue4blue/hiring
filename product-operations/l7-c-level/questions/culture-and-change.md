# Culture and Change

* Tell me about the largest change programme you have led. How long until it stuck?
* How do you diagnose culture through incentives rather than stated values?
* How do you communicate at company scale so that it lands?
* Tell me about leading through a contraction — layoffs or a cost programme.
* How do you manage change fatigue?
* How do you protect people's ability to say uncomfortable things to leadership?
* How does inclusion show up in your operating practice, concretely?
* What did you leave behind that is still working?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you personally say in the hardest meeting?"
* "What broke after you left, and why?"
