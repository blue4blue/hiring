# Strategy Execution and Governance

* How do you turn a company strategy into an executable plan with real owners?
* Describe your portfolio governance: stage gates, kill criteria, investment mix.
* Tell me about killing a major initiative. Who fought it?
* How do you manage company risk — operational, regulatory, security?
* Describe a crisis you commanded. What was the structure?
* What is in your board reporting on execution and risk?
* How do you hold the company to commitments it made a year earlier?
* Tell me about a benefit review that showed an investment did not pay off. What happened next?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who could overrule the kill decision, and did they?"
* "How fast did bad news reach you, and how did you engineer that?"
