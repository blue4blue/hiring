# Org Design and Talent

* Describe an organisational redesign you led. What was the rationale?
    * What did it cost, and what did you underestimate?
* How do you partner with a CEO on structure and accountability?
* How many operations leaders have you hired across functions? How did they do?
* How do you build an executive bench and a succession plan?
* Tell me about integrating an acquisition into the operating model.
* Tell me about exiting an executive.
* How do you think about culture as something the system produces?
* What decisions did you push the exec team to delegate?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did people say about that reorg six months later?"
* "Who from your org is running operations elsewhere now?"
