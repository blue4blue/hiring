# Culture and Change

- Leading company-wide change programmes and making them stick
- Diagnosing culture through what the system rewards, not what the posters say
- Communication at company scale: town halls, written narratives, cascades
- Building trust between leadership and the rest of the company
- Managing change fatigue and sequencing so the org can absorb it
- Leading through contraction: layoffs, cost programmes, morale
- Distributed and multi-site working models
- Inclusion as an operating practice, not a statement
- Protecting the ability to say uncomfortable things upward
- Leaving behind a system that works without you
