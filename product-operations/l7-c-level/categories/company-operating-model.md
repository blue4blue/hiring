# Company Operating Model

- Owning how the company executes: planning, resource allocation, decision-making, review
- Aligning product, engineering, go-to-market and finance operating rhythms into one system
- Strategy-to-execution translation and closing the gap between them
- Company planning architecture: annual plan, quarterly business review, weekly operating review
- Capital and headcount allocation processes
- Decision rights at executive level and escalation into the CEO
- Operating metrics for the whole company and their honest interpretation
- Scaling the model through growth, acquisition or contraction
- Removing organisational drag as deliberately as adding capability
- Knowing when the operating model cannot fix a strategy problem
