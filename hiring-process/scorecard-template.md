**Rating scale**

* **4 — Exceeds level.** Sets the bar; would raise the team's average.
* **3 — Meets level.** Solid, hire-worthy performance for this level.
* **2 — Below level.** Would need meaningful support; possible fit one level down.
* **1 — Well below level.** No usable signal or clearly wrong for the role.
