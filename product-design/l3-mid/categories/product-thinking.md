# Product Thinking

- Framing the design problem before opening Figma
- Understanding the business model and how design affects it
- Jobs to be done and outcome-based design goals
- Defining success metrics for a design change
- Scoping: the smallest design that tests the idea
- Trade-offs between user value, business value and feasibility
- Prioritising which parts of a journey deserve investment
- Designing for acquisition, activation, retention and monetisation differently
- Knowing when the answer is not a design change
- Arguing for design work in product terms, not aesthetic ones
