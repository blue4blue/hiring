# Research and Validation

- Choosing the method that fits the question and the risk
- Generative research: interviews, diary studies, contextual inquiry
- Evaluative research: moderated and unmoderated usability testing
- Concept testing and prototype fidelity decisions
- Quantitative UX: task success, SUS, funnel and event analysis
- Partnering with analysts on A/B tests of design changes
- Sample size, recruitment bias and the limits of five users
- Building and maintaining a research repository
- Triangulating research, analytics and support data
- Communicating uncertainty honestly
