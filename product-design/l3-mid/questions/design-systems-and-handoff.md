# Design Systems and Handoff

* Have you contributed to a design system? What did you add, and how was it decided?
* How would you structure tokens for a product that needs light, dark and a white-label theme?
* When should a pattern stay local to one team rather than going into the system?
* How do you design a component API — what would you expose as a variant versus a prop?
* How do you deprecate a pattern that 40 screens still use?
* What is in your handoff, and what do engineers still ask you about?
* How do you run design QA, and what do you block a release for?
* How do you make design debt visible to a PM who wants only new features?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who owned the system, and what was your relationship with them?"
* "What did you get wrong in that component's API?"
