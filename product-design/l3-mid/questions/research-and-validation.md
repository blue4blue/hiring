# Research and Validation

* How do you decide between an interview, a usability test and an A/B test?
* Tell me about a study you ran end to end. What was the question, and what did you conclude?
* You have one week and no research budget. How do you de-risk a major redesign?
* Five users found no problems. What do you actually know?
* How do you use analytics to decide where to look?
* Tell me about a time analytics and research disagreed.
* Have you designed a variant for an A/B test? What was the hypothesis, and what happened?
* How do you keep past research findable and used?
* How do you present findings when they contradict what leadership wants?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What would have falsified your hypothesis?"
* "Who did you recruit, and how were they biased?"
