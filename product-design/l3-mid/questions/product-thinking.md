# Product Thinking

* Take one portfolio project. What was the business problem behind the design problem?
    * What metric was it supposed to move? Did it?
* How do you decide what *not* to design?
* A PM asks for a redesign of the settings page. How do you decide whether it is worth doing?
* Tell me about a time the right answer was not a design change.
* How would you approach improving activation in our product?
    * What would you look at before designing anything?
* How do you scope a design so something ships in two weeks instead of two months?
* How do you argue for design investment to someone who sees design as decoration?
* What is the trade-off you make most often in your work?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the number before and after?"
* "What did the business give up to fund that work?"
