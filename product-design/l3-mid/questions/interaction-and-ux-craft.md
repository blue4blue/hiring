# Interaction and UX Craft

* Show me the most complex flow you have designed. Why was it complex?
    * Where does it break, and did you design that path?
* How do you handle a product with multiple roles and permissions?
* How would you restructure the information architecture of [an area of our product]?
* What changes in your design when the interface is translated into a language with 40% longer strings? Into an RTL language?
* Walk me through making a custom dropdown accessible.
    * Keyboard, screen reader, focus — what exactly do you specify?
* How does perceived performance change your design decisions?
* How do you design with real data instead of ideal data?
* Critique a flow in our product and propose a specific restructure.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you specify for focus management on close?"
* "What did you learn only after real users hit it?"
