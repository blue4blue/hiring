# Rubric — L3 Product Designer

A product designer owns design for a product area end to end. Assessed on **craft plus
product judgement**: the right design, for a stated reason, validated.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product thinking** | Design as execution of a brief | Frames the problem; ties design to a metric and a trade-off | Reframes the problem so the team builds something better |
| **UX craft** | Happy path, single locale, single role | Complex flows, roles, edge cases, WCAG AA by default | Systems-level design others reuse; catches ripple effects early |
| **Research** | Consumes research others run | Chooses and runs the right method; triangulates with data | Designs a validation plan for an ambiguous, high-risk bet |
| **Design systems** | Consumes, never contributes | Extends the system deliberately; thinks in component APIs | Improves the system's architecture, not just its inventory |
| **Collaboration** | Hands off finished screens | Shapes solutions with PM and engineering from the start | Trusted as an equal partner in product decisions |

## Must have

* Shipped work with an outcome they can describe, including one that did not work.
* Accessibility as a working habit, not a checklist at the end.
* Direct research experience they ran themselves.
* Fluency talking about feasibility with engineers.

## Nice to have

* Design system contribution or ownership.
* Experience in our platform mix (web + native, or B2B complexity).
* Content design or front-end skills.

## Red flags

* Portfolio has no constraints, no failures, and no post-launch story.
* Describes accessibility work as someone else's job.
* Cannot explain why a design decision was made beyond "it looked better".
* Every project was a redesign with no measured result.

## First 90 days if hired

Own an area, ship one measurable improvement, run research on the area's biggest
unknown, and contribute at least one pattern to the design system.
