# Business and Stakeholders

- Owning a design budget and headcount plan
- Making the commercial case for design investment
- Working with the CPO, CTO, CMO and CRO as peers
- Translating experience quality into business outcomes
- Reporting design performance to the exec team
- Agency and contractor management; build vs. buy for design capacity
- Handling commercial pressure that degrades the experience
- Customer and partner-facing representation
- Crisis: a launch that damages trust, an accessibility lawsuit, a public failure
- Change management when the company does not value design yet
