# Design Strategy and Vision

- Setting the experience vision for a business line and making it actionable
- Connecting design strategy to product and company strategy
- Deciding the differentiating experience: what you will be famous for
- Brand and product experience as one system
- Portfolio-level investment: features, foundations, system, research
- Multi-year experience roadmaps with quarterly value
- Competitive experience benchmarking
- Design's role in entering new markets or segments
- Ethics, trust and safety as design strategy, not compliance
- Communicating vision to the exec team and the company
