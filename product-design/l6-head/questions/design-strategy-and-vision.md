# Design Strategy and Vision

* What experience vision did you set, and how did it change what teams built?
* What was your business line famous for, experience-wise? Was that deliberate?
* How did design strategy connect to product strategy in your last company? Where did they conflict?
* How do you split investment between features, foundations, system and research?
* Tell me about a multi-year experience programme you led. How did it deliver value each quarter?
* How do you benchmark experience against competitors in a way that changes decisions?
* Where does ethics or trust show up in your design strategy?
* Based on what you can see, what should our product be famous for experientially?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you deliberately not invest in?"
* "How did you know the vision had been adopted rather than admired?"
