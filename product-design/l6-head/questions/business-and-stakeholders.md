# Business and Stakeholders

* What budget and headcount did you own?
* Make the commercial case for design investment to a CFO.
* Describe your relationship with your CPO and CTO. Where did you disagree?
* Tell me about commercial pressure that would have damaged the experience. What did you do?
* How do you report design's contribution to the exec team?
* When have you used agencies or contractors, and how did that go?
* Tell me about a public failure — a bad launch, a trust incident, an accessibility complaint.
* How do you change a company that treats design as a service function?
* What would you need from us in your first six months to be successful?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What number did you tie design work to, and did it move?"
* "What did you concede, and was it right?"
