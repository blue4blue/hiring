# Design Org and Hiring

* Describe the org you built: size, shape, specialisms, and why.
    * What would you change?
* When do you hire a researcher, a content designer, a systems designer, a design ops person?
* Embedded or centralised? Defend your answer for our situation.
* How many designers have you hired? What was your hit rate, and what did the misses share?
* Walk me through your portfolio review standard for a senior hire.
* How do you run promotion calibration for design so it is not subjective?
* Tell me about managing someone out.
* You inherit a team with no ladder, no calibration and inconsistent quality. First quarter?
* How do you keep senior ICs from leaving because the only path up is management?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Where are those hires now?"
* "What did you tolerate too long?"
