# Process and Quality Bar

* Describe how design work flows through your org today. Where does it get stuck?
* Who decides what ships when quality is contested?
* How do you run design review at org scale?
* How do you democratise research without the quality collapsing?
* How is your design system funded and governed?
* What accessibility standard do you hold, and what happens when a team misses it?
* How do you measure design? What is in your reporting?
* What process did you remove, and what happened?
* How do you keep designers close to users when the org grows?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you block from shipping, and what was the fallout?"
* "What did the metric actually show over four quarters?"
