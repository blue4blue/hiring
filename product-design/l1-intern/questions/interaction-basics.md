# Interaction Basics

* Sketch the flow for signing up and completing a first task in our product.
    * Where would people get stuck?
* Design a form to collect a delivery address. What do you include, and what do you leave out?
    * How do you handle a validation error?
* What should the screen show while data is loading? What if it takes 10 seconds?
* Design an empty state for a list a user has not filled in yet.
* When should you follow the platform convention, and when should you break it?
* What is progressive disclosure? Show me an example from a product you use.
* What is animation for, in a product interface?
* Rewrite this error message: "Error 402: request failed."
