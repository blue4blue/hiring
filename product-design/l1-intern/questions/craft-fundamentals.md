# Craft Fundamentals

* Walk me through one project in your portfolio. Start with the problem, not the screens.
    * What did you try that did not work?
* Here is a screen from our product. What is wrong with the hierarchy?
    * What would you change first, and why that first?
* How do you choose a type scale?
* What contrast ratio do you need for body text, and how do you check it?
* Show me a component you designed. What states did you define for it?
    * What happens when the data is empty? When it fails to load?
* How do you decide spacing between elements?
* What is the smallest touch target you would ship, and why?
* Redesign this button group for mobile. Talk me through the decisions.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Why that size and not one step up?"
* "What would a colour-blind user see here?"
