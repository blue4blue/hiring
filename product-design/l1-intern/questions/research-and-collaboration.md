# Research and Collaboration

* Have you ever watched someone use something you designed? What happened?
* You are taking notes in a usability session. What do you write down?
* A user says "I love it." What do you do with that?
* An engineer says your design is not feasible. What do you say next?
* A PM hands you a solution and asks you to "make it pretty." How do you respond?
* Tell me about critique you received that you disagreed with. What did you do?
* How do you critique someone else's work without being vague or harsh?
* You are blocked and your reviewer is unavailable for two days. What do you do?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did they actually do, as opposed to what they said?"
* "What was the question you should have asked earlier?"
