# Tools and Process

* Open a file from your portfolio. Show me how it is organised.
    * Could someone else pick this up tomorrow?
* Show me how you use auto-layout and components.
* When would you make something a component, and when not?
* You are using a design system that lacks the component you need. What do you do?
* What do you give an engineer at handoff?
* How do you check that what shipped matches what you designed?
* What do you know about how CSS lays out a page?
* How do you make a prototype you can actually test with a user in a day?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What would you rename in this file, and why?"
* "What did you decide *not* to specify, and how did that go?"
