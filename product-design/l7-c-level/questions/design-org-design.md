# Design Org Design

* Describe the largest design organisation you have led: size, shape, geography, specialisms.
* How many design directors or VPs have you hired? How did they work out?
* How do you decide what stays centralised and what is embedded?
* How do you handle a boundary dispute with a CPO or CTO over ownership?
* Tell me about exiting a senior leader. What did you do first?
* How do you build a bench so you are not the single point of failure?
* How does inclusion show up in your hiring and promotion practice, concretely?
* What decisions did you deliberately give away as the org grew?
* How do you keep senior ICs when the ladder gets crowded?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who from your org is leading design somewhere else now?"
* "What was the first signal that leader was not working, and how long did you wait?"
