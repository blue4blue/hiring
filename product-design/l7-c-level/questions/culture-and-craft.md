# Culture and Craft

* How are experience decisions made in your company today? Draw the actual path.
* How do you keep executives in contact with real users?
* How did you maintain the craft bar through growth or acquisition?
* Describe your working relationship with your CEO. Where did you disagree?
* Tell me about a trust failure or public backlash you led through.
* What has your company refused to build on ethical grounds? Were you the one who said no?
* How do you make the design case to the whole company, not just to leadership?
* What did you leave behind that is still working?
