# Company Experience and Brand Strategy

* What was the company's design point of view under your leadership? Say it in one sentence.
* How did you unify brand and product experience? What broke first?
* Tell me about a time design created structural advantage, not just improvement.
* How did you handle the experience of an acquired product with a different design language?
* What experience commitments did your company make publicly, and did you keep them?
* Tell me about entering a new market. What did the experience have to change?
* When did you tell the CEO that the strategy made good design impossible?
* Based on what you can see of us, what is our experience point of view — and what should it be?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you refuse to build, and what did that cost?"
* "How would a customer describe the difference?"
