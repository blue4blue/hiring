# Design Org Design

- Structuring design across business lines, geographies and acquired companies
- Hiring, developing and retaining design directors and VPs
- Succession planning and a bench two levels deep
- Levelling, compensation philosophy and calibration at scale
- Balancing centralised functions (system, research, brand, ops) with embedded teams
- Partnership with CPO and CTO as peers, including boundary disputes
- Inclusion in hiring, promotion and the work itself
- Executive performance management and exits
- Scaling craft culture without turning it into slogans
- Deciding which decisions to keep and which to hand away
