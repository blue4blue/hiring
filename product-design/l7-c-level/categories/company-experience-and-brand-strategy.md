# Company Experience and Brand Strategy

- Owning the experience the company delivers, end to end, across every touchpoint
- Brand and product as one continuous system: marketing, product, support, packaging
- Defining the company's design point of view and what it refuses to do
- Design as a source of structural advantage, not decoration
- Portfolio strategy across business lines and acquired products
- Experience implications of entering new markets, segments or geographies
- Trust, safety, privacy and accessibility as company-level commitments
- Long-range experience vision and how it survives quarterly pressure
- Public representation of the company's design position
- Knowing when the company's strategy makes good design impossible, and saying so
