# Rubric — L2 Junior Product Designer

A junior designer owns a feature or flow with direction. Assessed on **craft
fundamentals plus evidence they design for real conditions**, not just the happy path.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Visual craft** | Inconsistent, taste-driven, no system | Systematic and consistent; accessible by default | Craft that raises the bar of the work around it |
| **Interaction & flows** | Screens without a flow | Designs full flows including error, empty and edge states | Anticipates scale and failure conditions unprompted |
| **Research** | Relies on opinion | Has run usability sessions and acted on the findings | Chooses the right method and defends the sample honestly |
| **Collaboration** | Throws designs over the wall | Handoff engineers can build from; takes constraints seriously | Shapes the solution with engineering before it is drawn |
| **Communication** | Presents screens | Presents problem, options, rationale and the ask | Persuades a skeptical room with evidence |

## Must have

* Two or three portfolio projects they can explain end to end, including failures.
* Real usability testing experience, however informal.
* Fluency in Figma including components and auto-layout.
* Designs error, empty and loading states as a matter of course.

## Nice to have

* Contributions to a design system.
* Front-end literacy.
* Experience with a mobile platform as well as web.

## Red flags

* Portfolio of dribbble-style concepts with no shipped work and no constraints.
* Cannot describe what happened after launch.
* Accessibility described as "we do it at the end".
* Presents preference as evidence.

## First 90 days if hired

Own one feature end to end, run at least four usability sessions, and contribute one
component or pattern back to the design system.
