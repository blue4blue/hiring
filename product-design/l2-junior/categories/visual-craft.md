# Visual Craft

- Consistent application of a type scale and spacing system
- Colour systems: semantic tokens, light/dark, states
- Density and information hierarchy in data-heavy screens
- Layout across breakpoints, not just at two sizes
- Component composition and variant design
- Iconography, illustration and when each earns its place
- Motion: duration, easing, and communicating state change
- Accessibility: contrast, focus order, target size, reduced motion
- Visual polish under real content, not lorem ipsum
- Presenting rationale, not taste
