# Visual Craft

* Take me through the project you are most proud of. What was the problem?
    * Show me a direction you rejected. Why did you reject it?
* How is your type and spacing scale defined in that project?
* How do you approach dark mode? What breaks when you invert a palette naively?
* Show me a data-dense screen you designed. How did you manage density?
* What does your design look like with a 60-character name in that field?
* When do you use motion, and when is it decoration?
* How do you meet accessibility requirements without flattening the design?
* Critique this screen from our product on craft grounds alone.
