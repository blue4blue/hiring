# Collaboration and Handoff

* Show me how you hand a design to engineering. What is in the handoff?
* You need a component the design system does not have. What do you do?
* An engineer builds something that does not match the design. What happens next?
* How do you know whether something you are designing is expensive to build?
* Tell me about a time you changed your design because of a technical constraint.
* How do you present work in critique? What do you ask for?
* You are working on three features at once and all are due Friday. What do you do?
* How do you work with whoever writes the copy?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What questions did engineering still have to ask you?"
* "What did you push back on, and what did you accept?"
