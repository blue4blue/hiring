# Research and Usability

* How many usability sessions have you run yourself?
    * Walk me through the last one: the tasks, what you saw, what you changed.
* Write me a task for testing a checkout flow. Now tell me why it might be leading.
* Five users, three of them struggled with the same step. What do you conclude?
* A user says they prefer version A but succeeded faster with version B. Which do you ship?
* How do you recruit participants when you have no research team and no budget?
* How do you use analytics to decide where to test?
* Tell me about research that changed your design significantly.
* How do you present findings so the team actually acts on them?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you observe versus what did they report?"
* "What was the severity ranking, and who agreed with it?"
