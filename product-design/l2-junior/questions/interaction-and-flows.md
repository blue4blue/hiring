# Interaction and Flows

* Design the flow for [a common task in our product]. Sketch it out loud.
    * Where are the entry points? What happens if the user abandons halfway?
* How would you handle a destructive action — deleting something important?
* Design a filter experience for a list with 12 possible filters.
* What changes in your design when the list has zero results? Ten thousand?
* How do you design a multi-step form so people finish it?
* What do you do when the network is slow or the request fails?
* How do you decide between a modal, a drawer and a new page?
* Walk me through an onboarding you designed. Did it work? How did you know?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What does the user see one second after they tap it?"
* "Which of these states did you actually design, and which did engineering invent?"
