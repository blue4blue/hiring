# Design Direction and Portfolio

- Setting design direction across several teams or the whole product
- Deciding where craft investment pays off and where "good enough" is correct
- Multi-quarter design programmes with intermediate value
- Redesigns: when they are justified, and how to sequence them without a big bang
- Experience principles and design values that survive contact with deadlines
- Brand and product experience alignment
- Portfolio-level accessibility and quality standards
- Design's role in strategy conversations, before requirements exist
- Balancing platform work (system, tooling) against product work
- Making the case for design headcount and budget
