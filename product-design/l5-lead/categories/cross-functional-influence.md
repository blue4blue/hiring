# Cross-functional Influence

- Operating as a peer to product and engineering leadership
- Influencing strategy and roadmap before decisions are made
- Executive communication: business framing, not aesthetic framing
- Negotiating quality versus speed at portfolio level
- Working with research, data, content, brand, marketing and support
- Building trust across an org that has not valued design
- Handling political conflict without becoming political
- Advocating for users against short-term commercial pressure
- Representing design externally: hiring brand, conferences, customers
- Change management: introducing new ways of working
