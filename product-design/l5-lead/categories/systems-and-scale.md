# Systems and Scale

- Owning or governing a design system across many teams
- Token architecture, theming, white-labelling and multi-brand
- Contribution models: federated, centralised, hybrid
- Adoption: measuring it, driving it, and dealing with teams that opt out
- Versioning, deprecation and coordinated migration
- Design system as product: roadmap, users, support, documentation
- Tooling and automation: linting, code-connected components, token pipelines
- Accessibility remediation programmes at scale
- Design ops: research ops, file architecture, workflow, headcount planning
- Measuring design quality across a large surface area
