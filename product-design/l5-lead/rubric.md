# Rubric — L5 Lead / Staff Product Designer

At L5 the question is **leverage**: does the design work of many people get better
because this person is here? Two valid shapes — the systems/craft leader (Staff) and the
people-and-direction leader (Lead). Assess against the one you are hiring for.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Direction & portfolio** | Direction for one area | Sets direction across teams and defends the investment commercially | Redefined what the product's experience should be, and delivered it |
| **Systems & scale** | Contributed to a system | Owns or governs a system; drives adoption and migration | Built a system others in the industry could learn from |
| **Craft leadership** | Reviews work | Holds a bar across teams without bottlenecking; grows designers | Produced designers who now set the bar themselves |
| **Cross-functional influence** | Respected inside design | Peer to product and engineering leadership; shifts roadmaps | Changed how the company makes product decisions |
| **Judgement** | Optimises craft everywhere | Knows where craft pays and where it does not | Reallocates design effort to where the business actually needs it |

## Must have

* Named designers they developed, with trajectories.
* Work at portfolio or system scale, not just feature scale.
* A commercial argument they made and won.
* Evidence of choosing *not* to polish something.

## Nice to have

* Design system ownership at scale.
* Design ops experience.
* Both IC-depth and people-leadership phases.

## Red flags

* Leverage described only as "I reviewed everyone's work".
* Design system stories with no adoption numbers.
* Cannot name a place they accepted lower quality on purpose.
* Frames every cross-functional problem as other people not understanding design.

## First 90 days if hired

Publish a diagnosis of the experience and the system, set the craft bar through critique,
and pick the one systemic problem to fix in the following two quarters.
