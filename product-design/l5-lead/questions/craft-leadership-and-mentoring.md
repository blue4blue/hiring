# Craft Leadership and Mentoring

* How many designers have you mentored or managed? Where are they now?
* Tell me about someone whose craft you visibly changed. What did you do?
* Tell me about someone who did not improve. What happened?
* How do you hold a craft bar across teams without reviewing everything?
* How do you run critique at scale so it stays useful?
* What is the feedback you give most often to senior designers?
* How do you interview designers? What does your portfolio review look like?
* When do you take the pen yourself, and when do you stay out?
* How do you onboard a designer into a domain that takes six months to understand?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you say, in their words?"
* "What changed in their work, and over what period?"
