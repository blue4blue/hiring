# Cross-functional Influence

* Describe your relationship with your product and engineering counterparts.
    * Where did you disagree, and how did it resolve?
* Tell me about influencing a roadmap decision before design was involved.
* How do you present design work to an executive team?
* You are in an org that treats design as a service function. How do you change that?
* Tell me about a time you traded quality for speed. Was it the right call?
* How do you work with brand and marketing when the product experience diverges?
* Tell me about a political situation you handled badly. What would you do now?
* How do you advocate for users when the commercial pressure runs the other way?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did the other person get out of the outcome?"
* "How did the relationship look a year later?"
