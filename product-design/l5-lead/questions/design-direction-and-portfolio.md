# Design Direction and Portfolio

* Describe the scope you cover today. How many teams, how many surfaces?
* Where do you invest craft, and where do you deliberately accept "good enough"?
* Tell me about a redesign you led across multiple teams. How did you sequence it?
    * What did you refuse to do as a big bang, and why?
* What experience principles did you write, and did anyone use them?
* How do you argue for design headcount?
* Tell me about a strategic conversation you influenced before requirements existed.
* How do you balance design system investment against shipping features?
* What is the biggest experience risk you see in our product from the outside?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you stop investing in, and who complained?"
* "How did you measure that the direction was working?"
