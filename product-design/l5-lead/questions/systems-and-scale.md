# Systems and Scale

* Have you owned a design system? How many teams consumed it?
    * What was the adoption rate, and how did you measure it?
* Which contribution model did you use, and why?
* A team refuses to adopt the system because it slows them down. What do you do?
* How do you run a migration off a deprecated pattern across 20 teams?
* How do you architect tokens for multi-brand and multi-theme?
* What did you automate, and what stayed manual?
* Describe an accessibility remediation programme at scale. How did you prioritise?
* What design ops problems have you solved — file architecture, research ops, workflow?
* How do you measure design quality across a large product?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the adoption number before and after?"
* "What did the system get wrong that you had to undo?"
