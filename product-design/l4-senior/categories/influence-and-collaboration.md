# Influence and Collaboration

- Partnering with PM and engineering leads as an equal
- Influencing roadmap and strategy, not just execution
- Articulating design decisions to executives in business terms
- Running critique that raises quality without crushing people
- Mentoring designers and reviewing their work
- Negotiating scope, timelines and quality trade-offs
- Handling conflicting stakeholder direction
- Working with brand, marketing, support and sales
- Advocating for the user when the business pressure runs the other way
- Building trust with engineering through feasibility fluency
