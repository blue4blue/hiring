# Design Strategy

- Setting design direction for a core journey or complex area
- Connecting design work to business strategy and product bets
- Vision work: concept directions that shift what the team believes is possible
- Prioritising design investment across a portfolio of surfaces
- Defining experience principles the team can decide with
- Balancing consistency against differentiation
- Brand expression inside a product experience
- Making the case for foundational work (IA overhaul, system rebuild) with a business argument
- Knowing which problems design should not take on
- Planning multi-quarter design work with intermediate value
