# Complex Systems and Craft

* Describe the most complex system you have designed. What made it hard?
* How do you keep an experience coherent across web, iOS and Android without flattening each one?
* Tell me about a migration where users had to live with old and new simultaneously.
* How do you design for a moment where the user is handing over money or personal data?
* How do you approach designing an interface where the system is uncertain — a model that can be wrong?
* You inherit a product that fails an accessibility audit in 200 places. What is your plan?
* How does a performance budget change what you draw?
* Show me a data-dense interface you designed. How did you decide what to surface?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the failure case, and what did the user see?"
* "How did you validate that it worked at real scale?"
