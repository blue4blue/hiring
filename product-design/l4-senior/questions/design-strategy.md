# Design Strategy

* What design direction did you set that outlasted the project you set it in?
* Tell me about a vision concept you made. What did it change?
    * What did it cost, and was it worth it?
* How do you decide where to spend design effort across a whole area?
* Make the business case for rebuilding our information architecture.
* What experience principles did your team use to decide things without you in the room?
* When have you argued *against* a design investment?
* How do you balance consistency with differentiating a key moment?
* Where do you think our product's experience is weakest, based on what you have seen?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you not design in order to do that?"
* "How did leadership react, and what changed as a result?"
