# Research and Experimentation

* How do you validate a design bet that will take three quarters to deliver?
* Tell me about a design change you A/B tested. What was the hypothesis and the result?
    * What would you have done if the result had been flat?
* When is a design change too risky to A/B test?
* How do you measure whether the experience is getting better over time, not just per release?
* How do you research with enterprise users who cannot be recruited normally?
* Tell me about a study whose result you did not believe. What did you do?
* How would you build a research practice in a team that has none?
* How do you avoid measuring something gameable?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the pre-registered success criterion?"
* "How did you handle the segment where it lost?"
