# Influence and Collaboration

* Tell me about a time you changed a product decision, not just a design.
* Describe a disagreement with a PM you own. How did it end?
* How do you explain a design decision to a CFO or a CEO?
* Tell me about a designer you mentored. What did they do better afterwards?
* How do you run critique so that it raises quality and people still bring early work?
* You are asked to ship something you think harms users. What do you do?
* How do you handle two executives giving you opposite direction?
* How have you earned trust with an engineering team that was skeptical of design?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you prepare before that conversation?"
* "What did you concede?"
