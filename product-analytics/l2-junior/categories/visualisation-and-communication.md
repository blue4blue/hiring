# Visualisation and Communication

- Choosing the chart for the question, not for the aesthetics
- Axis, scale, baselines and honest visual encoding
- Colour, accessibility and avoiding rainbow palettes
- Small multiples and comparison design
- Dashboards people use vs. dashboards people ignore
- Writing an analysis: answer, evidence, caveat, recommendation
- Executive summaries and appendices
- Presenting uncertainty without hedging into uselessness
- Handling questions and challenges in a review
- Documenting so the analysis is reproducible
