# Analysis Fundamentals

- Turning a vague question into a testable one
- Exploratory analysis: shape, distribution, missingness, outliers
- Segmentation and finding where an average hides two behaviours
- Funnel and drop-off analysis
- Cohort analysis over time
- Root-cause investigation of a metric move
- Basic time-series awareness: seasonality, day-of-week, trend
- Estimating impact and sizing an opportunity
- Sanity checks and reconciliation against a second source
- Knowing when the data cannot answer the question
