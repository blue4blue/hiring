# Visualisation and Communication

* Show me a chart you made. Why that chart type?
* You have five segments over 24 months. How do you show it?
* When do you use a bar chart instead of a line?
* Show me a dashboard you built. Who used it, and how often?
    * How do you know?
* Write the first two sentences of an analysis email where the result is bad news.
* How do you communicate uncertainty without making the recommendation useless?
* A stakeholder challenges your number in a meeting. What do you do?
* How do you make an analysis reproducible by someone else?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did the reader actually do after reading it?"
* "What did you cut from the final version?"
