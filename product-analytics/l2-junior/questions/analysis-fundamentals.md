# Analysis Fundamentals

* Signups dropped 18% on Tuesday. Walk me through your investigation, step by step.
    * What are your first three hypotheses, and how do you rule each out cheaply?
* Conversion is flat overall but you suspect two segments moving in opposite directions. How do you check?
* How do you separate seasonality from a real trend?
* Estimate the impact of removing one step from our signup flow.
* You are asked "is feature X successful?" What do you ask back?
* How do you sanity-check a number before you send it?
* Tell me about an analysis where you concluded "we cannot answer this with the data we have."
* Tell me about an analysis you got wrong. How did you find out?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What would have falsified your hypothesis?"
* "Which cheap check would you run first, and why that one?"
