# Measurement Strategy

- Connecting product metrics to business outcomes and unit economics
- Designing a metric system for a business line, not a team
- Choosing the north star and defending it against fashionable alternatives
- Leading indicators for lagging business results
- Balancing growth, engagement, quality and monetisation metrics
- Metric ownership and accountability across teams
- Data trust: reconciliation with finance and other sources of record
- Building forecasting and target-setting into planning
- Analytics roadmap: what to instrument, model and automate next
- Deciding what not to measure
