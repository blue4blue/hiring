# Influence and Partnership

- Operating as a peer to PM and engineering leads, not as a query service
- Choosing which questions deserve depth and which deserve ten minutes
- Saying no to low-value requests without damaging the relationship
- Written analysis for executive audiences
- Presenting uncertainty to people who want certainty
- Challenging a decision that the data does not support
- Mentoring junior analysts and reviewing their work
- Teaching self-serve analytics with guardrails
- Working with data engineering on priorities
- Managing a queue and setting expectations
