# Measurement Strategy

* Design the metric system for our business line. What is the north star, and why not the obvious alternative?
* How do product metrics connect to revenue in your last company?
* What leading indicator did you find for a lagging business metric?
* How do you set a target for a metric with no history?
* Tell me about a metric you retired. Why, and what replaced it?
* How do you reconcile product numbers with finance numbers?
* What did you deliberately choose not to measure?
* Where does our analytics investment go next, based on what you can see?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who owned that metric, and what happened when it missed?"
* "How gameable was it, and did anyone game it?"
