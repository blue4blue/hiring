# Influence and Partnership

* Tell me about an analysis that changed a strategic decision.
* How do you decide which requests to do deeply and which to answer in ten minutes?
* How do you say no to a VP's request?
* Tell me about telling a leader their initiative did not work.
* How do you present a result with wide uncertainty to someone who wants a yes or no?
* Tell me about an analyst you mentored. What improved?
* How do you enable self-serve analytics without the numbers becoming unreliable?
* Describe a conflict with data engineering over priorities. How did it end?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did they do differently afterwards?"
* "What did you give up in that negotiation?"
