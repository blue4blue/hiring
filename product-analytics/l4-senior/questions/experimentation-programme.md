# Experimentation Programme

* How many experiments did your org run per quarter? What was the win rate?
    * What did you do about the losers?
* What needs an experiment in your world, and what does not?
* Describe your experiment review process. What has it caught?
* How do you measure cumulative impact across many small wins?
    * How do you handle the fact that individual wins do not add up to the annual metric?
* What validity checks run automatically before anyone sees a result?
* How do you run experiments in a low-traffic B2B product?
* How do you teach PMs to run tests without them misreading results?
* Have you audited past experiment decisions? What did you find?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What fraction of your 'wins' would survive a re-analysis?"
* "What did you change in the platform as a result?"
