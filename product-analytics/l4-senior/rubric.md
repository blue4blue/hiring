# Rubric — L4 Senior Product Analyst

A senior analyst owns measurement for a business-critical area and is trusted to choose
the method. Assessed on **methodological judgement and influence** — SQL is assumed.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Causal methods** | A/B tests only | Applies DiD, synthetic control or matching correctly and states assumptions | Designs a defensible measurement where randomisation is impossible |
| **Experimentation** | Runs tests well | Designs and governs a programme; catches validity failures | Audited past results and corrected the org's beliefs |
| **Measurement strategy** | Owns team metrics | Designs a metric system tied to business outcomes | Changed what the company optimises for, with evidence |
| **Influence** | Answers requests | Chooses what deserves depth; changes decisions | Trusted by executives as the arbiter of what is true |
| **Mentoring** | Reviews queries | Develops analysts with specific feedback | Raised the analytical bar of the whole team |

## Must have

* At least one non-experimental causal analysis they can defend in detail.
* A case where they told leadership something they did not want to hear.
* Experiment design experience including power and validity checks.
* Mentoring experience.

## Nice to have

* Analytics engineering ownership.
* Forecasting or planning ownership.
* Experience in a marketplace or network-effect product.

## Red flags

* Causal vocabulary without assumptions attached.
* Never refused a request.
* Every analysis confirmed what the team already believed.
* Treats the experimentation tool's output as the answer.

## First 90 days if hired

Audit the area's measurement and instrumentation, publish a metric system with owners,
raise the experiment bar, and deliver one analysis that changes a strategic decision.
