# Data Strategy and Vision

- Company data strategy: what data capability the business needs, in what order
- Data as a product and as an asset: valuation, leverage, defensibility
- AI and ML strategy: where models create value and where they create risk
- Analytics, data science, data engineering and ML platform as one operating system
- Buy, build and partner decisions at company scale
- Data monetisation and its ethical and regulatory limits
- Investment cases with multi-year payback to a board
- Consolidating data after acquisitions
- Deciding what the company will never do with data
- Making the company faster at deciding, not just richer in dashboards
