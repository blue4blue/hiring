# Org and Talent

- Structuring data across business lines, geographies and acquisitions
- Hiring and developing heads of analytics, data science and data engineering
- Succession planning and a bench two levels deep
- Levelling and compensation philosophy for scarce technical talent
- Central platform vs. embedded practitioners at company scale
- Partnership with CTO, CPO and CFO as peers
- Performance management at executive level
- Building analytical literacy as a company-wide capability
- Retention in a market where these people are always being recruited
- Culture: intellectual honesty, reporting your own errors, disagreeing with power
