# Governance, Privacy and Risk

- Company data governance: ownership, lineage, certification, audit
- Privacy regulation and consent architecture across jurisdictions
- PII minimisation, retention policy and deletion obligations
- Security partnership: access, breach preparation, incident response
- AI governance: model risk, bias, explainability, human oversight
- Regulatory reporting and audit readiness
- Third-party data sharing, contracts and liability
- Ethical limits: profiling, targeting, and what the company refuses to infer
- Board-level risk reporting on data
- Crisis: a breach, a regulator inquiry, a public data failure
