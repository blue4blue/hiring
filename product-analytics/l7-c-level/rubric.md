# Rubric — L7 Chief Data / Analytics Officer

Accountable for data across the company: strategy, platform, governance, risk and the
integrity of the numbers the business runs on.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Data strategy** | A tool stack roadmap | Sequenced capability strategy tied to business outcomes | Made data a durable source of advantage |
| **Org & talent** | Has run a large org | Hires and develops data executives; bench and succession | Alumni lead data organisations elsewhere |
| **Governance & risk** | Compliance checklist | Owns governance, privacy, AI risk and audit readiness | Led through a regulator inquiry or breach and strengthened the company |
| **Business** | Reports numbers | Accountable for forecasts and the data cost line | Built data products with real economics |
| **Board & exec** | Presents well | Peer to CTO/CPO/CFO; trusted with bad news | Shifts board understanding of data and AI without hype |
| **Integrity** | Reports what is asked | Protects the integrity of the numbers under pressure | Has taken a serious personal cost to do so |

## Must have

* Has hired, developed and exited data executives.
* Has owned company-level data governance and privacy.
* Has led through a data crisis.
* Has been accountable for a forecast and missed one honestly.
* References including a CEO, a CFO or CTO peer, and a former direct report.

## Nice to have

* Regulated-industry experience.
* Experience consolidating data through M&A.
* Credible AI governance experience, not just enthusiasm.

## Red flags

* AI talk with no risk framing.
* Governance described only as tooling.
* Cannot name a leader they built.
* Has never disagreed with a CEO about a number.

## Reference checks (mandatory at this level)

Ask a former CEO: "When did they tell you something you did not want to hear?"
Ask a CFO or CTO peer: "Did you trust their numbers, and why?"
Ask a former director: "What did they do when you made a serious mistake?"

## First 180 days if hired

Publish a diagnosis of data capability, governance and trust; fix the highest risk;
establish certified company metrics with owners; and commit to a capability sequence
with costs attached.
