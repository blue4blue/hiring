# Org and Talent

* Describe the largest data organisation you have led.
* How many heads of analytics, science or engineering have you hired? How did they do?
* How do you split platform from embedded practitioners at scale?
* How do you handle a boundary dispute with a CTO over data ownership?
* Tell me about exiting a data executive.
* How do you retain scarce talent when everyone is recruiting them?
* How do you build analytical literacy across a whole company?
* What decisions did you give away as the org grew?
* How do you build a culture where people report their own errors quickly?
