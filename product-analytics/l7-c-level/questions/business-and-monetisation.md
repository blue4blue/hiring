# Business and Monetisation

* What company numbers were you accountable for?
* How accurate were your forecasts, and what did you do when you missed?
* Tell me about a data or ML product that generated revenue. What were the economics?
* How do you manage the data cost line without destroying capability?
* How do you prove the return on data investment honestly?
* Describe working with a CFO who disputed your numbers.
* How do you communicate data and AI to a board without hype?
* You are told to cut the data budget 30%. What goes?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the actual payback, and did it hold?"
* "What did you promise the board that you did not deliver?"
