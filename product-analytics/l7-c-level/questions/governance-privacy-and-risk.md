# Governance, Privacy and Risk

* Describe your data governance model. Who owns what?
* How is consent architected across jurisdictions in your last company?
* Tell me about a privacy or security incident you led through.
* How do you govern AI models — bias, explainability, human oversight?
* Have you faced a regulator inquiry or audit? What happened?
* How do you handle third-party data sharing and its liability?
* What did your company refuse to infer or target? Who made that call?
* How do you report data risk to a board?
* What would you audit first here?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the exposure in numbers, and who assessed it?"
* "What changed structurally afterwards, and did it hold?"
