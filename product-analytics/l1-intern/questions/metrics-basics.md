# Metrics Basics

* Define retention. Now define it a second way. Which one would you use for our product, and why?
* What is the difference between DAU/MAU and retention?
* Conversion is 12%. What do you need to know to say whether that is good?
* Give me an example of a vanity metric and what you would use instead.
* A cohort chart shows week-4 retention at 18% for January and 24% for February. What do you check before celebrating?
* What does it mean if the retention curve flattens? What if it never flattens?
* How would you measure whether a new feature is "working"?
* A dashboard shows 0 events for yesterday. What are your first hypotheses?
