# Communication and Tools

* Summarise an analysis you have done in three sentences: answer, evidence, caveat.
* You have a time series over 18 months. What chart do you use, and why?
* When would you *not* start a y-axis at zero?
* A PM asks "why did signups drop yesterday?" What do you ask before you start?
* Write the structure of your analysis write-up. What are the sections?
* Show me something you have built in a spreadsheet, Python or a BI tool.
* Your analysis contradicts what the team expects. How do you present it?
* You are halfway through and realise the data is unreliable. What do you do?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What would you put in the first line of the email?"
* "What caveat would you refuse to leave out?"
