# Analytics Org and Hiring

* Describe the analytics org you built: size, shape, split of specialisms.
    * What would you change?
* Embedded, central or hybrid? Defend it for our situation.
* How do you split analytics engineering from product analytics?
* How many analysts have you hired? What was your hit rate?
* Walk me through your interview loop for a senior analyst.
* How do you make an IC track that seniors actually want?
* Tell me about managing someone out.
* Why do good analysts leave, in your experience, and what did you change about it?
* How do you build analytical literacy outside the analytics team?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Where are those hires now?"
* "What did you tolerate too long?"
