# Measurement and Governance

* Describe the company measurement framework you owned. Who owned each metric?
* How do you set targets in planning, and what happens when a team misses?
* How do certified metrics work in your org? Who can certify?
* Tell me about a data incident that reached the exec team.
* How do you handle PII and consent in your analytics stack?
* What is your data access policy, and how is it enforced?
* How do you reconcile product numbers with finance?
* What did you stop measuring, and how did you get agreement?
* How do you keep an experiment decision record that is actually used?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the regulatory exposure, and who assessed it?"
* "What changed structurally after that incident?"
