# Business Partnership

* What budget and headcount did you own?
* How did analytics change a business decision in your last role? Give the biggest example.
* How do you prioritise when demand is three times capacity?
* Tell me about presenting an uncomfortable result to the exec team.
* How do you translate uncertainty into a recommendation an executive can act on?
* Tell me about pressure to produce a favourable number. What happened?
* How do you report analytics' contribution — what is in your exec update?
* How do you partner with finance when your numbers disagree?
* What would you want from us in your first six months?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you refuse to do, and what did it cost you?"
* "How did the exec team's behaviour change?"
