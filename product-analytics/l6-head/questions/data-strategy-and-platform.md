# Data Strategy and Platform

* What was your data strategy, in order of capability?
* Tell me about a build-vs-buy decision on experimentation or BI. What did it cost, and was it right?
* How do you manage warehouse cost? What is the biggest reduction you have made?
* How do you enable self-serve without losing trust in the numbers?
* When is real-time worth the cost?
* Tell me about a migration you led. What was the honest overrun?
* How do you handle vendor lock-in?
* How do you decide the platform roadmap against product analytics demand?
* What technical debt in data have you deliberately carried, and why?
