# Business Partnership

- Partnering with product, engineering, finance, marketing and commercial leadership
- Turning analytics into a driver of decisions rather than a reporting function
- Owning a budget and headcount plan
- Reporting to the exec team: what to show and what to leave out
- Setting and defending targets in planning cycles
- Handling pressure to produce favourable numbers
- Translating analytical uncertainty into executive decisions
- Prioritising against demand that always exceeds capacity
- Communicating a data incident to the business
- Being the person who tells the company the truth
