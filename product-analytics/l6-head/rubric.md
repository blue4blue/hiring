# Rubric — L6 Head of Product Analytics

A Head of Analytics owns the analytics organisation, platform and standards for a
business line. Assessed on **the system and the truth-telling**, not on personal analyses.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Org & hiring** | Fills seats | Shapes the org; ladder, calibration, hiring standard | Built a bench; alumni lead analytics elsewhere |
| **Data strategy & platform** | Follows the modern-data-stack script | Sequenced capability plan with real cost management | Delivered a capability that changed the company's decision speed |
| **Governance** | Reactive quality fixes | Contracts, certified metrics, incident process, privacy discipline | Rebuilt company-wide trust in data measurably |
| **Business partnership** | Reporting function | Owns budget; changes business decisions; credible in planning | Analytics is a source of strategic advantage under them |
| **Integrity** | Reports what is asked | Delivers uncomfortable results with a plan | Has taken a real cost to protect the integrity of the numbers |

## Must have

* Has managed managers or leads.
* Has owned budget and platform spend.
* Has led a data incident with executive visibility.
* Has resisted pressure for a convenient number and can name the consequence.

## Nice to have

* Data engineering leadership experience.
* Experience in a regulated environment.
* Experience standing up analytics from near zero.

## Red flags

* Strategy described entirely as a tool stack.
* No governance or privacy story.
* Cannot name a leader they developed.
* Every improvement was a migration.

## First 90 days if hired

Publish a diagnosis of measurement, platform and team; fix the worst trust problem;
establish certified metrics with owners; and set the hiring and levelling standard.
