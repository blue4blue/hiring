# Experimentation and Statistics

* Design an A/B test for [a plausible change in our product]. Walk me through it end to end.
    * What is the OEC? What MDE, and why that number?
* How do you compute the required sample size? What do you do when the answer is six months?
* What is sample ratio mismatch, and what do you do when you see it?
* Why is peeking a problem, and what techniques let you look early legitimately?
* Explain CUPED to a PM. What does it buy you?
* You have 12 metrics and one is significant at p = 0.04. What do you conclude?
* When is the user the wrong randomisation unit?
* Your test wins for two weeks and the effect disappears after two months. What happened?
* A test is flat. What do you recommend?
