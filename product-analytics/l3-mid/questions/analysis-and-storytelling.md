# Analysis and Storytelling

* Tell me about an analysis that changed a product decision.
    * What was the decision before, and after?
* A VP asks "why is retention down?" You have two days. What do you do?
* How do you size an opportunity when there is no directly comparable data?
* Walk me through a churn driver analysis you have run.
* How do you present a result that contradicts a leader's stated belief?
* Tell me about an analysis you chose not to do. Why?
* How do you forecast, and how do you communicate the error bars?
* Tell me about a time you were confidently wrong.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What were your assumptions, and which one was load-bearing?"
* "What happened after you presented it?"
