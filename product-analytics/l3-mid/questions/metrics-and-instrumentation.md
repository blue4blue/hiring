# Metrics and Instrumentation

* Design a metric framework for [an area of our product]. What is the north star and why?
* What makes a metric gameable? Give an example you have seen.
* How do you choose between a sensitive metric and a meaningful one?
* You need to change a metric definition. History will not match. What do you do?
* How do you govern event instrumentation across ten teams?
* How do you handle cross-device identity in your measurement?
* Explain the limits of last-touch attribution to a marketing stakeholder.
* How do you detect a silent tracking failure — data still arrives but is wrong?
* Two teams report different numbers for the same thing in the same meeting. What do you do?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who owned that definition, and how was disagreement resolved?"
* "How long did the bad data go unnoticed, and what did you change afterwards?"
