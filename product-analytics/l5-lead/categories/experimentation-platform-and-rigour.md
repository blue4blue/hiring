- Owning the experimentation standard for the company
- Platform requirements: assignment, exposure logging, metric computation, guardrails
- Automated validity checks and blocking bad experiments

- Training programmes for PMs and engineers
- Measuring the health of the experimentation programme itself
- Post-hoc audits and correcting the record
