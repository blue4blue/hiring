# Data Quality and Governance

- Data contracts between product engineering and analytics
- Instrumentation review as part of the definition of done
- Testing, monitoring and alerting across the pipeline
- Incident process for data: detection, comms, backfill, postmortem
- Certified metrics, ownership and a single source of truth
- Semantic layer / metric store governance
- Documentation and discoverability that people actually use
- Access control, PII handling and privacy-by-design
- Data retention, consent and regulatory obligations
- Building trust: what to do after the company stops believing the numbers
