# Analytics Strategy

* What was your analytics agenda last year, as opposed to your request queue?
* How do you decide where analytics effort creates the most value?
* Tell me about a platform investment you argued for. How did you justify it?
* Buy or build for experimentation? Defend your answer with numbers.
* How do you balance embedded analysts with a central team?
* What data asset did you build that still matters two years later?
* What did you retire — dashboards, metrics, pipelines — and how did you handle the objections?
* Looking at us from outside, what would your first analytics priority be?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you say no to in order to do that?"
* "What was the measurable result of that investment?"
