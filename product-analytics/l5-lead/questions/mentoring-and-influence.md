# Mentoring and Influence

* How many analysts have you grown? Where are they now?
* What do you teach a junior analyst first, and why that?
* How do you review an analysis — what do you challenge?
* Tell me about being the only person in the room saying the result was not real.
* How do you influence a strategy discussion before anyone asks for data?
* How do you teach statistical literacy to product managers without condescension?
* Tell me about a time you were pressured for a convenient number.
* How do you build a culture where people report their own mistakes quickly?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you say, and what did they say back?"
* "What happened to your credibility afterwards?"
