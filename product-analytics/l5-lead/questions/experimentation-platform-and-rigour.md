# Experimentation Platform and Rigour

* What experimentation standard did you set, and how did you enforce it?
* What does your platform block automatically, and what does it merely warn about?
* How do you measure cumulative impact and reconcile it with the annual metric?
* Tell me about organisational pressure to declare a win. What did you do?
* How do you train PMs to run experiments without diluting rigour?
* How do you measure the health of the experimentation programme itself?
* Tell me about an audit of past experiments. What did you find, and how did you communicate it?
* Where does experimentation stop being the right tool in your judgement?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who could overrule you, and did they?"
* "What did that audit cost you politically?"
