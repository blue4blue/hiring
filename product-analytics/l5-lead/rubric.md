# Rubric — L5 Lead / Principal Product Analyst

At L5 the question is **leverage and standards**: does the whole organisation reach
better conclusions because this person is here?

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Analytics strategy** | Runs a request queue | Sets an agenda; invests in platform with a business case | Built a data capability that changed how the company decides |
| **Experimentation rigour** | Enforces best practice | Owns the standard and the platform; audits results | Corrected an organisation-wide false belief and made it stick |
| **Data quality & governance** | Fixes issues reactively | Contracts, monitoring, incident process, certified metrics | Rebuilt trust in data after it was lost |
| **Mentoring** | Reviews work | Grows analysts with a deliberate curriculum | Produced analysts who now set standards elsewhere |
| **Influence** | Respected by product teams | Peer to functional leadership; shapes strategy | Executives change direction based on their judgement |
| **Integrity** | Reports what is asked | Says the uncomfortable true thing | Has paid a real cost for being right and stayed effective |

## Must have

* Evidence of setting a standard that outlived them.
* Named analysts they developed.
* A time they resisted pressure for a convenient number.
* Platform or governance ownership, not just analysis.

## Nice to have

* Experience across both consumer and B2B measurement.
* Data engineering fluency.
* Experience rebuilding trust after a data failure.

## Red flags

* Leverage described only as "I answered more tickets".
* No governance or quality story at all.
* Has never disagreed with leadership on a result.
* Confuses tooling adoption with analytical maturity.

## First 90 days if hired

Publish a diagnosis of measurement, experimentation and data trust; set the
experimentation standard; and name the one platform investment worth making next.
