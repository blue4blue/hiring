# Positioning and Narrative

- Owning positioning for a product line and keeping it coherent
- Competitive alternatives framing, including "status quo" and "in-house build"
- Differentiation grounded in real product capability, not aspiration
- Narrative arc: the change in the world, the problem it creates, the resolution
- Messaging architecture across segments, personas and buying committee roles
- Naming and portfolio architecture
- Message testing with real audiences and iterating on the result
- Repositioning an existing product and managing the transition
- Aligning product, sales and marketing on one story
- Killing messages that test badly, including your own
