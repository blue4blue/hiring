# Sales Enablement and Content

* What does a seller need at each stage of a deal, and what did you build for each?
* Show me a battlecard you wrote. How do you know reps used it?
* How do you design training that changes behaviour rather than filling an hour?
* How do you measure enablement — what number moved?
* Tell me about a case study you produced end to end.
* Reps ignore your content and use their own decks. What is happening, and what do you do?
* How do you build an ROI or business-case tool for a buyer?
* How does enablement differ in a product-led motion?
* How do you retire content?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the adoption rate, and how did you measure it?"
* "What did the front-line reps say when you asked them directly?"
