# Positioning and Narrative

* Read me the positioning statement you are proudest of.
    * What were the competitive alternatives, and did you include "do nothing"?
* Tell me about repositioning a product. What triggered it, and how did you manage the transition?
* How do you build a messaging architecture across three personas without three products' worth of copy?
* What differentiator have you claimed that you later realised was not real?
* How do you test a message properly?
* Sales, product and marketing tell three different stories. What do you do?
* Write the narrative arc for our product in four sentences.
* What message did you kill? Why?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you exclude, and who complained?"
* "How did you know the new positioning was better — what moved?"
