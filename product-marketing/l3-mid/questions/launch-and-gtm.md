# Launch and Go-to-Market

* Walk me through a GTM plan you owned: segment, motion, channel, message, metric.
* How does GTM differ for a sales-led versus a product-led motion?
* Tell me about the largest launch you have led. Who was involved?
* How do you sequence channels for a launch?
* How do you decide the launch tier, and who agrees?
* How do you handle a launch where pricing changes at the same time?
* What happens in the 30 days after launch in your plans?
* Tell me about a launch that underperformed. What did you learn, and what changed?
* What is your launch scorecard?
