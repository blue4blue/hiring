# Messaging Basics

- Features vs. benefits vs. outcomes
- Value proposition: for whom, what problem, what result
- Audience and segment: writing for one person, not everyone
- Headline, subhead, proof — the basic structure of a claim
- Proof points: data, customer quotes, demonstrations
- Tone of voice and consistency
- Plain language and cutting jargon
- Calls to action that match the reader's stage
- Adapting one message across channels
- Editing your own writing down by a third
