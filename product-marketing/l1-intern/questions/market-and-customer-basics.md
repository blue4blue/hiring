# Market and Customer Basics

* Who is the customer, the buyer and the user for our product? Are they the same person?
* Name three competitors and one non-obvious alternative.
* How would you research a competitor in an afternoon? What sources?
* What makes a persona useful rather than decorative?
* Walk me through the buying journey for a product you have researched or bought.
* What questions would you ask a customer to understand why they chose us?
* How would you roughly size the market for [a plausible product]?
* What would you look at in product analytics to see whether a launch worked?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you learn that you could not have got from their website?"
* "What is the strongest thing a competitor does better?"
