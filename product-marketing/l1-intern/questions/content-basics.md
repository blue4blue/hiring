# Content Basics

* Show me something you have written. Walk me through the choices in it.
* Write the outline of a landing page for [a feature of our product].
* Write a subject line and first two sentences of a launch email.
* When would you write a blog post rather than a one-pager?
* Write a release note for a change that improves speed by 30%.
* What SEO considerations would you apply to a product page?
* How would you know whether a piece of content worked?
* Here is a draft with three claims. Which one would you cut, and why?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What do you want the reader to do after reading it?"
* "What did you cut from your own draft?"
