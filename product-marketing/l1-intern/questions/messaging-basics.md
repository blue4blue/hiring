# Messaging Basics

* Take our product. Write a one-sentence value proposition for [a specific segment].
    * Now write it for a different segment. What changed and why?
* What is the difference between a feature, a benefit and an outcome? Give an example from our product.
* Here is a paragraph of product copy. Cut it by half without losing the meaning.
* What proof would make that claim believable?
* Write a headline and subhead for our homepage.
* This message says "the leading AI-powered platform for modern teams." What is wrong with it?
* How would you adapt one message for an email, a landing page and a sales deck?
* Whose problem does our product solve, and what were they doing before?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who would read that and think it is not for them? Is that intentional?"
* "What is the specific claim, and how would you prove it?"
