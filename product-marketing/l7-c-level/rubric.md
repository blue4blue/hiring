# Rubric — L7 Chief Marketing Officer

Accountable for the company's story, brand and marketing's contribution to revenue.
Assessed on **narrative judgement at company scale, commercial accountability, the
leaders they build, and honest claims**.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Narrative & brand** | Campaign-level thinking | Owns a company story others adopt; defends the brand/demand split | Built brand equity the market and analysts repeat back |
| **Org design** | Has run a large org | Hires and develops VPs; bench and succession | Alumni run marketing organisations elsewhere |
| **Growth & revenue** | Reports channel metrics | Accountable for contribution; uses incrementality, not just attribution | Changed the growth model or motion economics |
| **Exec & board** | Presents well | Peer to CEO/CPO/CRO/CFO; credible with bad news | Shifts board thinking about the market |
| **Culture & talent** | Sets standards | Builds an evidence-and-writing culture | Left a marketing organisation that outperformed after departure |
| **Integrity** | Follows legal review | Refuses claims that cannot be substantiated | Took a real cost to keep the company's claims honest |

## Must have

* Has hired, developed and exited marketing executives.
* Has been accountable for a revenue or pipeline number and can state it.
* Has led through a reputation crisis or a major downturn.
* Can name a claim they refused to make.
* References including a CEO, a CRO or CFO peer, and a former direct report.

## Nice to have

* Experience across both enterprise and self-serve motions.
* Rebrand, pivot or M&A narrative experience.
* Public voice that helps recruiting and analyst standing.

## Red flags

* Attribution claims with no incrementality thinking.
* Brand work with no commercial framing, or performance work with no brand view.
* Cannot name a leader they built.
* Every failure attributed to product or sales.

## Reference checks (mandatory at this level)

Ask a former CEO: "Did their story match what the company could actually deliver?"
Ask a CRO or CFO peer: "Did their numbers hold up?"
Ask a former direct report: "What happened when you brought them bad news?"

## First 180 days if hired

Publish a diagnosis of the company narrative, brand position and growth model; make the
leadership calls that are already clear; agree a contribution number with the CEO and
CRO; and rewrite the company story with evidence behind every claim.
