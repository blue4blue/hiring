# Marketing Org Design

- Structuring marketing across product marketing, demand, brand, content, comms and ops
- Centralisation versus embedded by business line or geography
- Hiring and developing VPs and heads across marketing disciplines
- Succession planning and executive bench
- Levelling, compensation philosophy and calibration
- Marketing operations, martech stack and data infrastructure
- Agency strategy and in-house versus outsourced capability
- Performance management at executive level
- Culture: evidence over opinion, writing, customer contact
- Deciding which decisions the CMO keeps
