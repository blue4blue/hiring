# Company Narrative and Brand

* What was the company story under you? Say it in one sentence.
* How did you align product, sales, recruiting and investor narratives?
* Have you led a rebrand or a pivot's narrative? What did it cost, and did it work?
* What was your brand-versus-performance split, and how did you defend it to a CFO?
* Tell me about a reputation crisis you led through.
* Tell me about a time the product could not support the claim. What did you do?
* How do you measure brand honestly?
* Based on what you can see of us, what is our story — and what should it be?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you stop saying, and who objected?"
* "What moved over 12 months, and how did you attribute it?"
