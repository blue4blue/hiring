# Growth and Revenue Model

* What revenue or pipeline contribution were you accountable for? Did you hit it?
* Walk me through the company growth model. What was the binding constraint?
* How do you allocate budget across brand, demand and PMM, and what evidence supports it?
* How do you handle attribution at company scale? Have you run incrementality tests?
* Tell me about a monetisation or pricing change you partnered on. What happened?
* How do you manage marketing efficiency under cost pressure without destroying the pipeline?
* What did you commit to in planning, and what happened when you missed?
* How do you communicate growth to a board without hype?
