# Marketing Org Design

* Describe the largest marketing organisation you led: size, shape, geography.
* How do you structure across PMM, demand, brand, content, comms and ops?
* How many VPs or heads have you hired? How did they do?
* How do you decide what is centralised and what is embedded?
* Tell me about exiting a marketing executive.
* What is your agency strategy, and what do you insist on keeping in-house?
* How do you build a bench so you are not the single point of failure?
* How does inclusion show up in your hiring and in the work itself?
* What decisions did you give away as the org grew?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who from your org runs marketing somewhere else now?"
* "What was the first signal that hire was not working?"
