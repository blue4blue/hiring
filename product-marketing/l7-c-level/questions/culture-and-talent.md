# Culture and Talent

* How do you build a marketing culture grounded in evidence rather than opinion?
* How do you keep executives in contact with real customers?
* How do you make writing a company capability?
* Describe your relationship with your CEO and CRO. Where did you disagree?
* Tell me about a claim you refused to make.
* Tell me about leading marketing through a downturn or layoffs.
* How do you develop marketing leaders?
* What did you leave behind that is still working?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you say in the hardest meeting?"
* "What broke after you left, and why?"
