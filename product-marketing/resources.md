# Resources

## Books

### Positioning and narrative

* Obviously Awesome, by April Dunford
* Sales Pitch, by April Dunford
* Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind, by Al Ries and Jack Trout
* Building a StoryBrand, by Donald Miller
* Play Bigger, by Ramadan, Peterson, Lochhead and Maney

### Go-to-market and launch

* Crossing the Chasm, by Geoffrey Moore
* Loved: How to Rethink Marketing for Tech Products, by Martina Lauchengco
* Product-Led Growth, by Wes Bush

### Pricing and monetisation

* Monetizing Innovation, by Madhavan Ramanujam and Georg Tacke
* The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing, by Thomas Nagle

### Research and customers

* The Mom Test, by Rob Fitzpatrick
* Competing Against Luck, by Clayton Christensen
* Demand-Side Sales 101, by Bob Moesta

### Writing

* On Writing Well, by William Zinsser
* Everybody Writes, by Ann Handley
* Made to Stick, by Chip and Dan Heath
