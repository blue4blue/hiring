# Rubric — L2 Associate Product Marketing Manager

Executes launches and content with direction. Assessed on **writing quality, customer
contact and reliable launch execution**.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Positioning & messaging** | Feature lists in prose | Writes a real positioning statement with proof points | Chooses a segment to lose deliberately and defends it |
| **Research** | Repeats what sales says | Talks to customers directly; runs basic win/loss and competitive work | Finds an insight that changed positioning |
| **Content** | Produces assets on request | Writes conversion-focused copy and measures it | Copy that outperforms and gets reused across the company |
| **Launch** | Runs a checklist | Owns a launch plan across functions; enables sales and support | Rescued a launch after a late change |
| **Reliability** | Deadlines slip quietly | Delivers on time; flags risk early | Trusted with the tier-1 launch |

## Must have

* Writing samples that show clarity and specificity.
* Direct customer conversations, not just internal input.
* At least one launch they owned a meaningful part of.
* Numbers attached to something they produced.

## Nice to have

* Win/loss experience.
* SEO or lifecycle marketing skills.
* B2B sales enablement or self-serve growth experience — say which you need.

## Red flags

* All messaging is adjectives with no proof.
* Never spoken to a customer.
* Launch stories with no post-launch metric.
* Cannot say who the product is not for.

## First 90 days if hired

Own messaging for one product area, run two launches end to end, complete ten customer
or win/loss conversations, and rewrite one core asset with measured results.
