# Positioning and Messaging

- Positioning: competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, best-fit customer
- Messaging hierarchy: positioning statement, pillars, proof points, boilerplate
- Segment-specific messaging without fragmenting the brand
- Differentiation that survives a competitor reading it
- Objection handling built into the message
- Naming features and products
- Message testing: with customers, with sales, with traffic
- Keeping messaging current as the product changes
- Message consistency across channels and teams
- Writing the one-liner, the paragraph and the page from the same core
