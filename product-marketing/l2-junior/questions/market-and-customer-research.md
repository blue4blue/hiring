# Market and Customer Research

* How many customer calls did you do last quarter? What did you ask?
* Have you run win/loss? Walk me through what you found and what changed.
* How do you keep competitive intelligence current without it becoming a chore?
* What is on a battlecard you have written?
* How do you segment a market? Which segmentation actually changed a decision for you?
* What do review sites tell you that customers will not say directly?
* How would you size the market for [a plausible new product]?
* Tell me about research that changed the positioning.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "Who did you talk to, and how were they biased?"
* "What did sales believe that turned out to be wrong?"
