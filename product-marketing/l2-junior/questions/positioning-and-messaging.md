# Positioning and Messaging

* Show me a positioning document you wrote. Read me the statement itself.
    * Who was it *not* for?
* Write positioning for our product against our closest competitor.
* What are the components of a positioning framework you use?
* How do you handle a message that sales likes and customers do not respond to?
* How do you test messaging? What have you actually tested?
* A competitor claims the same differentiator you do. What do you do?
* How do you keep messaging consistent when five teams write copy?
* Take this feature and give me the one-liner, the paragraph and the page headline.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What is the proof point, and where did the number come from?"
* "What did the customer actually say, in their words?"
