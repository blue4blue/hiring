# Content and Campaigns

* Show me the best thing you have written and tell me how it performed.
* Design a content plan for a launch: what assets, for whom, in what order?
* Write the headline and first paragraph of a product page for [our feature].
* How do you source and write a customer story? What is the hardest part?
* What SEO work have you done on product or comparison pages?
* How do you work with demand generation? Where does the handoff go wrong?
* How do you measure whether content worked?
* You have one week and need five assets. What do you do?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What were the numbers — traffic, conversion, influenced pipeline?"
* "What did you stop producing because it did not work?"
