# Launch Support

* Walk me through a launch you ran. What was in the plan?
* How do you tier launches, and what changes between tiers?
* What do sales and support need before launch, and when?
* Tell me about a launch that went badly. What was the cause?
* Product cuts a headline feature three days before launch. What do you do?
* How do you run a beta or early-access programme's communications?
* What do you measure after launch, and for how long?
* What did your last launch retro change?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was adoption at 30 days, and was that good?"
* "What did you personally own versus the rest of the team?"
