# Rubric — L5 Lead / Principal Product Marketing Manager

At L5 the question is **leverage over the market story and the commercial model**: does
the company's narrative, pricing and GTM get better because this person is here?

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Portfolio GTM** | Owns one product's GTM | Allocates across a portfolio with channel economics | Changed the company's motion mix and can show the effect |
| **Narrative & category** | Product messaging | Company narrative others adopt; credible analyst strategy | Shifted how the market describes the space |
| **Monetisation** | Communicates pricing | Owns price architecture, modelling and migration | Changed monetisation with a measured NRR or ARPU effect |
| **Mentoring** | Reviews work | Defines the bar and grows PMMs deliberately | Produced PMMs who now set the bar elsewhere |
| **Influence** | Respected by sales | Peer to product, sales and marketing leadership | Changes what the company builds and charges |

## Must have

* Portfolio-level ownership, not a single product.
* A pricing or packaging change they led with numbers.
* Named PMMs they developed.
* A commercial commitment they were held to.

## Nice to have

* Category or analyst-relations track record.
* Both PLG and enterprise motion experience.
* International or multi-currency pricing experience.

## Red flags

* Narrative work with no measurable market effect.
* Pricing described only as a communications exercise.
* Never refused a sales request.
* Cannot name someone they made better.

## First 90 days if hired

Publish a portfolio GTM and narrative diagnosis with economics, set the PMM quality bar
through review, and name the one monetisation or motion change worth making.
