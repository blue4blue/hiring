# Mentoring and Influence

- Growing PMMs and defining what good looks like in a fuzzy discipline
- Setting the writing and research bar across a team
- Reviewing positioning and messaging work without rewriting it
- Interviewing PMMs and calibrating other interviewers
- Operating as a peer to product, sales and marketing leadership
- Influencing the roadmap with market evidence
- Saying no to sales requests that would damage positioning
- Executive communication and board-adjacent narrative work
- Building relationships with analysts, press and key customers
- Being the person who tells the company what the market actually thinks
