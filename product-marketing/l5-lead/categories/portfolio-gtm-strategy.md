# Portfolio GTM Strategy

- Owning go-to-market across a portfolio of products and segments
- Allocating marketing investment across products, segments and channels
- Motion design: which products are sold, which are self-serve, which are partner-led
- Cross-sell, upsell and portfolio attach strategy
- Market expansion sequencing: segment, vertical, geography
- Channel portfolio economics and diminishing returns
- Aligning GTM with product strategy and roadmap sequencing
- Forecasting commercial outcomes and being held to them
- Retiring products and migrating their customers
- Deciding which markets to leave
