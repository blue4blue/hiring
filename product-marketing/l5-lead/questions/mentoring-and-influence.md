# Mentoring and Influence

* How many PMMs have you grown? Where are they now?
* What does good PMM work look like to you? How do you communicate that bar?
* How do you review someone's positioning without rewriting it?
* Tell me about influencing the roadmap with market evidence.
* Tell me about refusing a sales leader's request. How did it end?
* How do you present market reality to an executive team that does not want to hear it?
* How do you build relationships with analysts and key customers?
* How do you interview PMMs? What is the exercise you always use?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What feedback did you give, in their words?"
* "What did the product team build differently because of you?"
