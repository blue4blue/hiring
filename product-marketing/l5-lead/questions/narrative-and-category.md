# Narrative and Category

* What was your company narrative, and how did product narratives ladder into it?
* Have you attempted category creation? What did it cost, and did it work?
* Describe your analyst relations strategy. Did you influence evaluation criteria?
* How do you get the whole company telling one story?
* Tell me about a competitor whose narrative beat yours. What did you do?
* How do you write a narrative that survives more than two quarters?
* How did you handle the narrative in a downturn or after bad news?
* How do you measure narrative effectiveness?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What moved — inbound quality, win reasons, analyst placement?"
* "What did you stop claiming, and why?"
