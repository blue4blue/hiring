# Portfolio GTM Strategy

* Describe the portfolio you covered. How did you allocate marketing investment across it?
* Which products were sold and which were self-serve? How did you decide?
* Tell me about a cross-sell or attach strategy you built. What were the numbers?
* How did you sequence expansion — segment, vertical or geography first?
* Where did a channel stop scaling, and what did you do?
* Tell me about retiring a product and migrating its customers.
* How do you align GTM readiness with a roadmap that keeps moving?
* What market did you decide to leave, or should have?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was under-invested, and what did that cost?"
* "What did you commit to commercially, and did you hit it?"
