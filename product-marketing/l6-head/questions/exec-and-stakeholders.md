# Executive and Stakeholder Leadership

* Describe your relationship with the CRO and CPO. Where did you disagree?
* Tell me about sales pressure that would have distorted positioning. What did you do?
* How do you get product, sales and marketing onto one plan?
* How do you communicate market position to a board?
* Tell me about a repositioning that changed how everyone worked. How did you manage it?
* How have you used agencies, and how did that go?
* Describe a customer advisory board you ran. Was it useful?
* How do you make the whole company fluent in the story?
* What would you need from us in your first six months?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you concede, and was it right?"
* "How did the relationship look a year later?"
