# Marketing Strategy and Narrative

* Describe the marketing strategy you owned. What was the audience, narrative and channel mix?
* How did you split budget between brand and demand? Defend the split.
* Tell me about a narrative you set that the whole company adopted.
* What was your analyst and press strategy? What did it achieve?
* How do you handle quarterly pipeline pressure against multi-year narrative investment?
* Tell me about a competitor who out-narrated you.
* How do you measure brand?
* Tell me about a crisis communication you led.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you stop funding to do that?"
* "What actually moved, and over what period?"
