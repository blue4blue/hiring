# Revenue and Pipeline Impact

* What number were you accountable for? Did you hit it?
* Walk me through your funnel model. Where was the constraint?
* How do you handle attribution honestly when leadership wants a single number?
* How do you allocate budget across channels, and what was payback?
* Tell me about improving win rate or deal velocity. What did marketing actually change?
* In a product-led motion, what did you own — signup, activation, conversion?
* How did pricing changes show up in your funnel?
* How do you work with revenue operations when your numbers disagree?
* What is in your exec report, and what did you remove from it?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What were the actual numbers before and after?"
* "What did you claim credit for that you were not sure about?"
