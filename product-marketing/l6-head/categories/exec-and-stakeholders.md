# Executive and Stakeholder Leadership

- Partnering with the CPO, CRO and CEO as peers
- Aligning product, sales and marketing on one plan and one story
- Handling sales pressure that would distort positioning
- Executive and board communication about market position
- Cross-functional launch governance
- Managing agencies, contractors and external partners
- Customer advisory boards and reference programmes
- Internal communication: making the company fluent in the story
- Change management when repositioning affects everyone's work
- Being the source of truth about what the market thinks
