# Rubric — L6 Head of Product Marketing

Owns the product marketing organisation, the narrative and a commercial contribution for
a business line. Assessed on **the story, the team and the number**.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategy & narrative** | Aggregates product messaging | Sets a business-line narrative with a defended budget split | Made the narrative a competitive asset the market repeats |
| **Org & hiring** | Fills seats | Shapes the org; ladder, calibration, writing-based hiring standard | Built a bench; alumni lead PMM orgs elsewhere |
| **Revenue impact** | Reports activity | Owns a pipeline or revenue number and forecasts credibly | Changed win rate, NRR or motion economics measurably |
| **Executive partnership** | Serves sales | Peer to CRO and CPO; holds positioning under pressure | Changed what the company builds and how it sells |
| **Talent judgement** | Avoids hard calls | Manages performance fairly and promptly | Raises the writing and thinking bar of everyone around them |

## Must have

* Has managed managers or leads.
* Has owned a commercial number.
* Has held positioning against sales pressure and can name the cost.
* Has built a hiring standard that includes real writing assessment.

## Nice to have

* Both enterprise and PLG motion experience.
* Analyst relations at scale.
* International market experience.

## Red flags

* Attribution claims with no acknowledgement of their limits.
* Narrative achievements with no market evidence.
* Cannot name a PMM they developed.
* Describes the role as supporting sales.

## First 90 days if hired

Publish a market and narrative diagnosis, fix the hiring and quality bar, agree a
commercial number with the CRO, and rewrite the core narrative with evidence behind it.
