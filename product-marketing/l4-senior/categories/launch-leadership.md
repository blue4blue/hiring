# Launch Leadership

- Leading launches spanning product, marketing, sales, support, legal and partners
- Go/no-go criteria and the authority to stop a launch
- Launch programmes across geographies and regulatory regimes
- Enablement at scale: certification, train-the-trainer, field readiness
- Analyst, press and customer-reference programmes
- Crisis handling when a launch goes wrong publicly
- Sequencing announcements to protect the narrative
- Executive and board communication around launches
- Measuring the launch portfolio, not just individual launches
- Institutionalising launch learning
