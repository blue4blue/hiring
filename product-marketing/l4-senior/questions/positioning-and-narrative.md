# Positioning and Narrative

* Tell me about positioning a portfolio rather than a single product. What was hard?
* Have you tried to create or redefine a category? What actually happened?
* What is the market shift your narrative rested on? Was it real?
* Where did you concede ground to a competitor deliberately?
* How do you position for a buying committee with five different concerns?
* Tell me about influencing an analyst's evaluation criteria.
* Tell me about handling the narrative after a competitor's major launch or after bad press.
* How do you measure whether a narrative is working?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What changed in win rates or inbound after the reposition?"
* "What did you have to stop saying?"
