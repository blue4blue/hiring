# Go-to-Market Strategy

* Describe a GTM strategy you owned. What was the motion, and why that one?
* Tell me about entering a new segment or geography. What did you get wrong?
* How do you compare channel economics? What did the numbers say?
* How does GTM change when the same company runs sales-led and product-led motions?
* How do you sequence a year of launches so they build a story rather than compete?
* Tell me about a partner or ecosystem GTM you built.
* How do you forecast the commercial effect of a GTM change?
* Tell me about a motion you shut down.

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was CAC and payback by channel?"
* "What did sales leadership say, and how did you resolve it?"
