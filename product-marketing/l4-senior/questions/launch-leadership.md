# Launch Leadership

* Describe the largest launch you have led: functions, geographies, duration.
* What are your go/no-go criteria, and have you ever stopped a launch?
* How do you run enablement for a large field organisation?
* Tell me about an analyst or press programme you ran around a launch.
* A launch causes a public problem. Walk me through the first day.
* How do you sequence announcements so they build rather than dilute?
* How do you report launch performance to executives?
* How do you measure a portfolio of launches rather than one?
* What did your launch process learn last year that it did not know before?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What did you personally decide, as opposed to coordinate?"
* "What did the 90-day numbers say, honestly?"
