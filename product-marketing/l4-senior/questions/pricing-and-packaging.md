# Pricing and Packaging

* Tell me about a pricing or packaging change you led. What was the process?
    * What happened to churn, ARPU and win rate?
* How do you research willingness to pay?
* How do you choose a price metric, and what did you learn from choosing badly?
* A new feature is ready. Include it, upsell it or make it a SKU? How do you decide?
* How do you migrate existing customers to new pricing?
* How do you handle discounting discipline with a sales team under quota pressure?
* When is a pricing experiment inappropriate?
* How do you work with finance on margin implications?

## Follow-ups to test depth

* "What was the realised price versus list, before and after?"
