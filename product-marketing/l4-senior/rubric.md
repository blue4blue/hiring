# Rubric — L4 Senior Product Marketing Manager

Owns GTM strategy for a major product or segment, with real influence over pricing and
launch decisions. Assessed on **commercial judgement and cross-functional leadership**.

## Competencies and the bar

| Competency | Below level (1–2) | At level (3) | Above level (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GTM strategy** | Executes an existing motion | Chooses segment, motion and channel with economics behind it | Changed the motion and can show the commercial result |
| **Positioning & narrative** | Product-level messaging | Portfolio narrative tied to a real market shift | Shifted how the market or analysts describe the space |
| **Pricing & packaging** | Communicates pricing | Runs WTP research; owns a packaging decision with finance | Changed monetisation with measured effect on ARPU and churn |
| **Launch leadership** | Coordinates launches | Leads multi-function, multi-geography launches; can stop one | Rescued or stopped a launch and was proved right |
| **Influence** | Serves sales requests | Peer to sales and product leadership; shapes the roadmap | Changed what the company built or charged |

## Must have

* A pricing or packaging decision they were central to.
* GTM strategy ownership with numbers.
* Evidence of stopping or reshaping something, not just executing.
* Direct customer and win/loss contact at volume.

## Nice to have

* Analyst relations track record.
* Multi-geography or regulated launch experience.
* Experience in both PLG and enterprise motions.

## Red flags

* Pricing described only as what marketing communicated.
* Category-creation talk with no evidence anything changed.
* Never disagreed with sales leadership.
* All launch metrics are activity metrics.

## First 90 days if hired

Publish a GTM diagnosis with channel economics, rewrite the portfolio narrative with
evidence, and take ownership of the next tier-1 launch end to end.
